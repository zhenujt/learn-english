# 16 Ellipsis, Substitution, and Parallelism

## Definition and Core Formula

Omit recoverable repetition: `I can help, and she can too`. Use `one/ones` for countable nouns and `do/does/did` for repeated actions. Keep items in a list in the same grammatical form.

## Progressive Examples

1. `I prefer tea, and she does too.` 2. `We need a faster server than the one we use.` 3. `The study aims to identify, evaluate, and measure.`

## Common Mistakes

`reading, to write, and coding` -> `reading, writing, and coding`. `I think so it works` -> `I think so`.

## Self-check and Answers

Shorten: `I can review it, and Mia can review it too.` Answer: `I can review it, and Mia can too.`
