# 14 条件句与虚拟语气

## 定义与核心公式

条件句表达真实可能、假设或对过去的遗憾。零条件：`If + present, present`；第一条件：`If + present, will + V`；第二条件：`If + past, would + V`（与现在/未来事实相反或可能性低）；第三条件：`If + had + V-ed, would have + V-ed`（与过去事实相反）。

虚拟语气还出现在建议/要求后：`suggest/request/require that + S + (should) + V`；`It is essential that + S + V`。

## 递进例句

1. `If I have time, I will call you.`
2. `If I were the project lead, I would postpone the release.`
3. `Had the team tested the migration, it would have avoided the outage.`

## 常见错误

| 易错 | 正确 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `If I would be you...` | `If I were you...` | `if` 从句不用 `would` 表假设。 |
| `If I knew, I will tell you.` | `If I knew, I would tell you.` | 第二条件主从句配对。 |
| `I suggest him to go.` | `I suggest that he go.` / `I suggest going.` | `suggest` 的常见结构不同。 |

## 自测与答案

1. 补全：`If we ___ (have) more time, we ___ (test) every case.` 2. 改为第三条件：`We did not back up the data, so we lost it.` 3. 写一条正式建议。答案：1. `had; would test`。2. `If we had backed up the data, we would not have lost it.`
