# 15 倒装与强调句

## 定义与核心公式

倒装通过把助动词/连系动词放在主语前突出信息：`Never have I seen ...`，`Only after ... did we ...`。否定或限制副词在句首时常倒装。强调句固定为：`It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who + 其余部分`。不要把普通疑问句和强调句混淆。

## 递进例句

1. `It was my colleague who helped me.`
2. `Only after we checked the logs did we find the cause.`
3. `Not only did the intervention improve accuracy, but it also reduced completion time.`

## 常见错误

| 易错 | 正确 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `Only then we understood.` | `Only then did we understand.` | 限制副词句首要倒装。 |
| `It is in Beijing where we met.` | `It is in Beijing that we met.` | 强调句用 `that/who`。 |
| `Never I have seen ...` | `Never have I seen ...` | 助动词在主语前。 |

## 自测与答案

1. 倒装：`We understood the risk only after deployment.` 2. 强调“yesterday”：`We fixed the issue yesterday.` 3. 用 `Not until` 造句。答案：1. `Only after deployment did we understand the risk.` 2. `It was yesterday that we fixed the issue.`
