# 15 Inversion and Emphasis

## Definition and Core Formula

After a negative or limiting phrase at the beginning, invert the auxiliary and subject: `Never have I seen ...`; `Only after ... did we ...`. Use `It is/was + focused element + that/who + remainder` to focus information.

## Progressive Examples

1. `It was my colleague who helped.` 2. `Only after checking logs did we find the cause.` 3. `Not only did accuracy improve, but time also fell.`

## Common Mistakes

`Only then we understood` -> `Only then did we understand`. `It is in Beijing where` -> `It is in Beijing that`.

## Self-check and Answers

Focus `yesterday` in `We fixed it yesterday`: `It was yesterday that we fixed it.`
