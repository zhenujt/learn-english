# 14 Conditionals and the Subjunctive

## Definition and Core Formula

Use `If + present, will + V` for likely future conditions, `If + past, would + V` for unreal present/future conditions, and `If + had + V-ed, would have + V-ed` for unreal past conditions. Use `suggest that + subject + base verb` for formal recommendations.

## Progressive Examples

1. `If I have time, I will call.` 2. `If I were the lead, I would postpone the release.` 3. `Had the team tested it, it would have avoided the outage.`

## Common Mistakes

`If I would be you` -> `If I were you`. `If I knew, I will tell` -> `If I knew, I would tell`.

## Self-check and Answers

`If we ___ more time, we ___ every case.` Answer: `had; would test`.
