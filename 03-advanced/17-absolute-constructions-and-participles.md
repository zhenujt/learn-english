# 17 Absolute Constructions and Participles

## Definition and Core Formula

Use participle phrases to compress a time, reason, or condition: `Having finished the test, we deployed`. An absolute construction has its own noun: `The test completed, we deployed`. Keep the logical subject clear.

## Progressive Examples

1. `Feeling tired, I went home.` 2. `With the configuration updated, the team restarted the service.` 3. `All variables controlled, the researchers attributed the difference to the intervention.`

## Common Mistakes

`Driving to work, the rain started` -> `Driving to work, I saw the rain`. The participle phrase must describe the main subject.

## Self-check and Answers

Compress: `Because the database was backed up, we started the migration.` Answer: `With the database backed up, we started the migration.`
