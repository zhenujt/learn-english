# 18 Formal Writing and Review

## Definition and Core Formula

Build a paragraph with `topic sentence + explanation/evidence + example/impact + transition`. Use logical links precisely: `however`, `therefore`, `for example`, and `furthermore`. Prefer specific verbs and measurable nouns.

## Progressive Examples

1. `The update saves time.` 2. `The update reduces manual work; however, it needs a migration window.` 3. `The evidence suggests that automated checks improve reliability; therefore, they should be integrated before deployment.`

## Common Mistakes

`In my opinion, I think` -> choose one. Do not use `although` and `however` for the same contrast.

## Self-check and Answers

Write 80--120 words with a passive sentence, a conditional, a clause, and a linker. Check that every clause has a subject and verb, every pronoun has a clear reference, and clause word order is declarative.
