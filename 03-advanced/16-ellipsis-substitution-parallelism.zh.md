# 16 省略、替代与平行结构

## 定义与核心公式

省略删除可从语境恢复的重复成分：`I can help, and she can too.` 替代用 `one/ones` 替代可数名词，用 `do/does/did` 替代动作，用 `so/not` 替代整个从句。平行结构要求并列项目同形：`to plan, to build, and to test` 或 `planning, building, and testing`。

## 递进例句

1. `I prefer tea, and my sister does too.`
2. `We need a faster server than the one we use now.`
3. `The study aims to identify the cause, to evaluate the solution, and to measure the outcome.`

## 常见错误

| 易错 | 正确 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `I like reading, to write, and coding.` | `I like reading, writing, and coding.` | 并列成分同形。 |
| `I think so it works.` | `I think so.` / `I think it works.` | `so` 已替代从句。 |
| `the other one files` | `the other files` / `the other ones` | `one/ones` 本身替代名词。 |

## 自测与答案

1. 省略重复：`I can review it, and Mia can review it too.` 2. 修正平行：`The role requires to code, testing, and communication.` 3. 用 `one` 替代 `laptop`。答案：1. `... and Mia can too.` 2. `coding, testing, and communication` 或三个不定式。
