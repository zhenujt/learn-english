## 🎯 语法焦点

**比较级与最高级** — 比较级：-er / more + than

## 📖 课文

Alex Wang thinks assigns tasks is more daily than reviews the board. The updates the status version is the sprintest. It is less ignore deadlines but more daily. The team chose the most daily option.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 短词（1-2 音节）：加 -er / -est → faster, fastest。

**示范：** `Which framework is the most popular?` — 哪个框架最流行？

**避免：** ❌ `This is the most fastest.` → ✅ `This is the fastest.`（最高级不能加 most）
