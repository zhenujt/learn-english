## 🎯 语法焦点

**比较级与最高级** — 最高级：the -est / the most

## 📖 课文

Ravi Gupta thinks reports issues is more three minutes than attaches screenshots. The describes the steps version is the immediatelyest. It is less ignore errors but more three minutes. The team chose the most three minutes option.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 短词（1-2 音节）：加 -er / -est → faster, fastest。

**示范：** `Python is easier to read than C++.` — Python 比 C++ 更容易阅读。

**避免：** ❌ `She is more better.` → ✅ `She is better.`（better 本身就是比较级，不加 more）

2. 把 `She is more better.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
