# Lesson 103: Encouragement / 鼓励

> **Book 1** · Unit 9: 健康与情绪 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**将来时：be going to / will** — be going to vs will 区别 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Tomorrow, Ravi Gupta is going to try again. The release will happen on learn from mistakes. We are not going to compare yourself to others this sprint. Ravi Gupta will give up after one failure as soon as possible.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** be going to + 动词原形：已计划好的将来行动。I am going to deploy tomorrow.

**示范：** `She isn't going to attend the demo.` — 她不打算参加演示。

**避免：** ❌ `I will going to help.` → ✅ `I am going to help. / I will help.`（不要混用两个结构）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"鼓励"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I will going to help.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
