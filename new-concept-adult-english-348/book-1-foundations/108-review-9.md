# Lesson 108: Review 9 / 第九单元复习

> **Book 1** · Unit 9: 健康与情绪 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**情态动词：can / should / must** — can / can't 表能力和许可

## 📖 课文

Lin Wei can prioritize health quickly. Lin Wei should set boundaries before the meeting. The team must overwork by Friday. You shouldn't ignore warning signs without testing. Can you communicate needs?

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** can 表示能力或许可：I can write Python. Can I leave early?

**示范：** `We must finish this before Friday.` — 我们必须在周五前完成。

**避免：** ❌ `I must to go.` → ✅ `I must go.`（must 后直接加原形）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"第九单元复习"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I must to go.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
