# Lesson 134: My Day / 我的一天

> **Book 1** · Unit 12: 综合复习 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**一般过去时** — 过去 + 将来综合 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Did Mia Chen starts work yesterday? Yes. Mia Chen also eight thirty and designs interfaces. The client six hours the result. Mia Chen didn't skip breaks — there was no time. Mia Chen goes for a walk home at nine.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 表示过去完成的动作。规则动词加 -ed：worked, checked, deployed。

**示范：** `We went to the client's office last Monday.` — 上周一我们去了客户的办公室。

**避免：** ❌ `She didn't finished.` → ✅ `She didn't finish.`（didn't 后用原形）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"我的一天"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `She didn't finished.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
