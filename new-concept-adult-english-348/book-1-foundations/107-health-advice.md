# Lesson 107: Health Advice / 健康建议

> **Book 1** · Unit 9: 健康与情绪 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**情态动词：can / should / must** — can / can't 表能力和许可

## 📖 课文

"Can I drink more water?" "Yes, you can." "James Hu must rely on energy drinks today." "You should also eat regular meals." "James Hu can't skip breakfast right now — the system is down."

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** can 表示能力或许可：I can write Python. Can I leave early?

**示范：** `You should back up your files.` — 你应该备份文件。

**避免：** ❌ `You should to check it.` → ✅ `You should check it.`（should 后不加 to）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"健康建议"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `You should to check it.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
