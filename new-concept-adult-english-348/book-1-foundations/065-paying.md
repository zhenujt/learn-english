# Lesson 065: Paying / 支付

> **Book 1** · Unit 6: 购买与服务 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**可数与不可数名词 / some & any** — 不可数名词 + how much / how many

## 📖 课文

How much cash does Alex Wang need? Not much. How many receiptss are there? There are five. Alex Wang doesn't need any information today. Could I have some change, please?

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 可数名词有单复数：a task / tasks; an error / errors。

**示范：** `Do you have any questions?` — 你有问题吗？

**避免：** ❌ `She has many softwares.` → ✅ `She has a lot of software.`（software 不可数）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"支付"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `She has many softwares.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
