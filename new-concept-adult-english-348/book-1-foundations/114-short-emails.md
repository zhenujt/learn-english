# Lesson 114: Short Emails / 简短邮件

> **Book 1** · Unit 10: 电话与消息 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**情态动词：can / should / must** — must / mustn't / have to 表义务

## 📖 课文

Yuki Tanaka can send a brief update quickly. Yuki Tanaka should by 5 PM before the meeting. The team must attach the file by Friday. You shouldn't forget the subject line without testing. Can you proofread before sending?

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** can 表示能力或许可：I can write Python. Can I leave early?

**示范：** `She can't access the database.` — 她无法访问数据库。

**避免：** ❌ `I must to go.` → ✅ `I must go.`（must 后直接加原形）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"简短邮件"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I must to go.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
