## 🎯 语法焦点

**过去进行时** — when + 过去时 / while + 过去进行时

## 📖 课文

Yuki Tanaka was complete the audit when the next month happened. While the team was schedule a follow-up, someone drop everything. Yuki Tanaka wasn't prioritize the backlog at that moment. Were you document the decision when the alert came?

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 结构：was/were + 动词-ing。表示过去某一时刻正在进行的动作。

**示范：** `He wasn't paying attention during the meeting.` — 他开会时没在注意听。

**避免：** ❌ `She were sleeping.` → ✅ `She was sleeping.`（she 搭配 was）
