# Lesson 045: Maps / 地图

> **Book 1** · Unit 4: 地点与出行 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**介词：in / on / at / to / from** — 时间介词：at 9 AM / on Monday / in June

## 📖 课文

David Zhou arrives at the office at eleven. The meeting room is on the second floor. David Zhou puts the laptop on the desk. The coffee machine is in the parking lot. After work, David Zhou goes to the museum.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 时间：at + 钟点 (at nine); on + 日/星期 (on Monday); in + 月/年/早午晚 (in June, in the morning)。

**示范：** `He is from the QA team.` — 他来自质量保证团队。

**避免：** ❌ `She is on the meeting.` → ✅ `She is in the meeting.`（会议是抽象空间用 in）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"地图"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `She is on the meeting.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
