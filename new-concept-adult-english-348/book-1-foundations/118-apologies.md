# Lesson 118: Apologies / 道歉

> **Book 1** · Unit 10: 电话与消息 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**将来时：be going to / will** — 将来时 + 情态动词综合 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Nina Zhao is going to apologize sincerely next right away. The team will explain what happened after the review. Nina Zhao won't make excuses until the tests pass. "Will you offer a solution?" "Yes, I will."

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** be going to + 动词原形：已计划好的将来行动。I am going to deploy tomorrow.

**示范：** `She isn't going to attend the demo.` — 她不打算参加演示。

**避免：** ❌ `I will going to help.` → ✅ `I am going to help. / I will help.`（不要混用两个结构）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"道歉"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I will going to help.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
