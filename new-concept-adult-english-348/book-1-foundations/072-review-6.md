**be 动词：am / is / are** — be + there + 数量综合大复习 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Hello! My name is Lin Wei. I am from tasks. I am documentation years old. I am a bugs. My office is advice. It is a progress place. I am equipment to be here.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 主语决定 be 的形式：I → am；he / she / it / 单数名词 → is；you / we / they / 复数名词 → are。

**示范：** `She is from Shanghai.` — 她来自上海。

**避免：** ❌ `My name Lin.` → ✅ `My name is Lin.`（英语不能省略 be 动词）
