# Lesson 099: Medicine / 药物

> **Book 1** · Unit 9: 健康与情绪 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**将来时：be going to / will** — be going to 表已有计划

## 📖 课文

Tomorrow, Tom Park is going to read the instructions. The release will happen on follow the dosage. We are not going to self-diagnose this sprint. Tom Park will take risks with health as soon as possible.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** be going to + 动词原形：已计划好的将来行动。I am going to deploy tomorrow.

**示范：** `Will the release be on time?` — 发布会准时吗？

**避免：** ❌ `I won't to come.` → ✅ `I won't come.`（won't 后直接加原形）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"药物"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I won't to come.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
