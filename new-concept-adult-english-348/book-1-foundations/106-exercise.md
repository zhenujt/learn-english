# Lesson 106: Exercise / 运动

> **Book 1** · Unit 9: 健康与情绪 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**情态动词：can / should / must** — can / can't 表能力和许可

## 📖 课文

Nina Zhao can walk during lunch quickly. Nina Zhao should use the stairs before the meeting. The team must sit all day by Friday. You shouldn't skip all movement without testing. Can you find an exercise buddy?

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** can 表示能力或许可：I can write Python. Can I leave early?

**示范：** `I can speak English and Chinese.` — 我会说英语和中文。

**避免：** ❌ `She can speaks English.` → ✅ `She can speak English.`（情态动词后用原形）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"运动"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `She can speaks English.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
