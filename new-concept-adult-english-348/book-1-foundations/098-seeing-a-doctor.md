# Lesson 098: Seeing a Doctor / 看医生

> **Book 1** · Unit 9: 健康与情绪 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**将来时：be going to / will** — be going to 表已有计划

## 📖 课文

Mia Chen is going to rest more next reduce screen time. The team will complete the deadline after the review. Mia Chen won't ignore the pain until the tests pass. "Will you ask for sick leave?" "Yes, I will."

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** be going to + 动词原形：已计划好的将来行动。I am going to deploy tomorrow.

**示范：** `She isn't going to attend the demo.` — 她不打算参加演示。

**避免：** ❌ `She is going to goes.` → ✅ `She is going to go.`（going to 后用原形）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"看医生"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `She is going to goes.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
