# Lesson 042: Asking Directions / 问路

> **Book 1** · Unit 4: 地点与出行 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**介词：in / on / at / to / from** — 地点介词：in the room / on the desk / at the office

## 📖 课文

Yuki Tanaka arrives at the office at three. The meeting room is on the fifth floor. Yuki Tanaka puts the laptop on the desk. The coffee machine is in the lobby. After work, Yuki Tanaka goes to the hotel.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 时间：at + 钟点 (at nine); on + 日/星期 (on Monday); in + 月/年/早午晚 (in June, in the morning)。

**示范：** `I work in a small office.` — 我在一间小办公室工作。

**避免：** ❌ `She is on the meeting.` → ✅ `She is in the meeting.`（会议是抽象空间用 in）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"问路"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `She is on the meeting.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
