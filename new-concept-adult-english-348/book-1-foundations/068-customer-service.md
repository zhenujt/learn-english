# Lesson 068: Customer Service / 客服

> **Book 1** · Unit 6: 购买与服务 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**可数与不可数名词 / some & any** — some/any 在肯定/否定/疑问中 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Emma Liu needs some issues. There isn't any patience left. "Do you have any tickets?" "Yes, here are two ticketss." Emma Liu also wants a piece of knowledge. There is a lot of research to review.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 可数名词有单复数：a task / tasks; an error / errors。

**示范：** `Could I have some water?` — 我能喝点水吗？

**避免：** ❌ `She has many softwares.` → ✅ `She has a lot of software.`（software 不可数）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"客服"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `She has many softwares.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
