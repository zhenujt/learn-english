# Lesson 092: Calendar / 日历

> **Book 1** · Unit 8: 计划与邀请 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**一般过去时** — 过去时态综合 ⟲（过去时 + 过去进行时） 🔄

## 📖 课文

Did Emma Liu book the room yesterday? Yes. Emma Liu also tomorrow afternoon and invite stakeholders. The client double-book the result. Emma Liu didn't check availability — there was no time. Emma Liu confirm attendance home at nine.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 表示过去完成的动作。规则动词加 -ed：worked, checked, deployed。

**示范：** `She did not reply to the email.` — 她没有回复那封邮件。

**避免：** ❌ `She didn't finished.` → ✅ `She didn't finish.`（didn't 后用原形）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"日历"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `She didn't finished.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
