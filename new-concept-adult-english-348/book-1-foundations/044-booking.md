# Lesson 044: Booking / 预订

> **Book 1** · Unit 4: 地点与出行 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**介词：in / on / at / to / from** — 时间介词：at 9 AM / on Monday / in June

## 📖 课文

At seven, Emma Liu is in the lounge. The keyboard is on the desk. The charger is in the bag. Emma Liu goes to the cinema at nine thirty. The parking lot is behind the building.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 时间：at + 钟点 (at nine); on + 日/星期 (on Monday); in + 月/年/早午晚 (in June, in the morning)。

**示范：** `She goes to the gym after work.` — 她下班后去健身房。

**避免：** ❌ `See you in Monday.` → ✅ `See you on Monday.`（星期用 on）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"预订"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `See you in Monday.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
