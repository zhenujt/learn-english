# Lesson 102: Feelings / 感受

> **Book 1** · Unit 9: 健康与情绪 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**将来时：be going to / will** — will 表临时决定、预测和承诺

## 📖 课文

Yuki Tanaka is going to express concerns next listen actively. The team will hide emotions after the review. Yuki Tanaka won't dismiss feedback until the tests pass. "Will you be patient?" "Yes, I will."

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** be going to + 动词原形：已计划好的将来行动。I am going to deploy tomorrow.

**示范：** `Don't worry, I will fix it.` — 别担心，我会修复的。

**避免：** ❌ `I won't to come.` → ✅ `I won't come.`（won't 后直接加原形）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"感受"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I won't to come.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
