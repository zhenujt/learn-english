# Lesson 086: Next Week / 下周

> **Book 1** · Unit 8: 计划与邀请 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**过去进行时** — 过去进行时：was/were + doing

## 📖 课文

Mia Chen was start the redesign when the Wednesday happened. While the team was present the mockup, someone postpone the demo. Mia Chen wasn't finish wireframes at that moment. Were you send updates when the alert came?

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 结构：was/were + 动词-ing。表示过去某一时刻正在进行的动作。

**示范：** `I was testing the feature when the server crashed.` — 我在测试功能时服务器崩了。

**避免：** ❌ `While I coded, the phone rang.` → ✅ `While I was coding, the phone rang.`（while 引导的持续动作用进行时）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"下周"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `While I coded, the phone rang.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
