# Lesson 104: Taking Leave / 请假

> **Book 1** · Unit 9: 健康与情绪 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**将来时：be going to / will** — be going to vs will 区别 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Emma Liu is going to submit the form early next notify the team. The team will leave without telling anyone after the review. Emma Liu won't forget the handover until the tests pass. "Will you plan coverage?" "Yes, I will."

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** be going to + 动词原形：已计划好的将来行动。I am going to deploy tomorrow.

**示范：** `Will the release be on time?` — 发布会准时吗？

**避免：** ❌ `She is going to goes.` → ✅ `She is going to go.`（going to 后用原形）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"请假"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `She is going to goes.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
