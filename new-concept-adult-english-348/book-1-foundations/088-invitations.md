# Lesson 088: Invitations / 邀请

> **Book 1** · Unit 8: 计划与邀请 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**过去进行时** — when + 过去时 / while + 过去进行时

## 📖 课文

Sara Li was join the workshop when the Friday happened. While the team was prepare materials, someone cancel the event. Sara Li wasn't coordinate logistics at that moment. Were you send reminders when the alert came?

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 结构：was/were + 动词-ing。表示过去某一时刻正在进行的动作。

**示范：** `While she was presenting, someone asked a question.` — 她在演示时有人提了问题。

**避免：** ❌ `I was work when he called.` → ✅ `I was working when he called.`（过去进行时需要 -ing）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"邀请"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I was work when he called.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
