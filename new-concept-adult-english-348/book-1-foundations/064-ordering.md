# Lesson 064: Ordering / 点餐

> **Book 1** · Unit 6: 购买与服务 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**可数与不可数名词 / some & any** — 不可数名词 + how much / how many

## 📖 课文

Sara Li needs some juice. There isn't any rice left. "Do you have any salads?" "Yes, here are two saladss." Sara Li also wants a piece of help. There is a lot of work to review.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 可数名词有单复数：a task / tasks; an error / errors。

**示范：** `She gave us two pieces of advice.` — 她给了我们两条建议。

**避免：** ❌ `I need an information.` → ✅ `I need some information.`（information 不可数）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"点餐"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I need an information.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
