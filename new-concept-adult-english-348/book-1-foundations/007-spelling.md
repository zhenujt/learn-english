# Lesson 007: Spelling / 拼读

> **Book 1** · Unit 1: 建立第一句话 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**be 动词：am / is / are** — be 否定句：I am not late. It isn't ready.

## 📖 课文

Ravi Gupta is not alone. There are many people in the large office. Ravi Gupta is from Mumbai. Ravi Gupta is thirty-three. The manager is confident. Everything is new and interesting.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 主语决定 be 的形式：I → am；he / she / it / 单数名词 → is；you / we / they / 复数名词 → are。

**示范：** `She is from Shanghai.` — 她来自上海。

**避免：** ❌ `I is tired.` → ✅ `I am tired.`（I 永远搭配 am）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"拼读"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I is tired.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
