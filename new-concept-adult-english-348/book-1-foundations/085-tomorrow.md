# Lesson 085: Tomorrow / 明天

> **Book 1** · Unit 8: 计划与邀请 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**过去进行时** — 过去进行时：was/were + doing

## 📖 课文

At 3 PM yesterday, Lin Wei was deploy the hotfix. The server was review the design normally. Suddenly, the Monday occurred. While Lin Wei was attend the retro, a colleague skip the standup.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 结构：was/were + 动词-ing。表示过去某一时刻正在进行的动作。

**示范：** `He wasn't paying attention during the meeting.` — 他开会时没在注意听。

**避免：** ❌ `I was work when he called.` → ✅ `I was working when he called.`（过去进行时需要 -ing）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"明天"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I was work when he called.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
