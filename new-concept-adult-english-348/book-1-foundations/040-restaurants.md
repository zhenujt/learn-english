# Lesson 040: Restaurants / 餐厅

> **Book 1** · Unit 4: 地点与出行 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**介词：in / on / at / to / from** — 地点介词：in the room / on the desk / at the office

## 📖 课文

The file is on Sara Li's desktop. The team works in a room on the first floor. They have lunch at twelve thirty. Sara Li walks from the office to the office. The report is in corner table's folder.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 时间：at + 钟点 (at nine); on + 日/星期 (on Monday); in + 月/年/早午晚 (in June, in the morning)。

**示范：** `He is from the QA team.` — 他来自质量保证团队。

**避免：** ❌ `I wake up in 7 AM.` → ✅ `I wake up at 7 AM.`（钟点用 at）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"餐厅"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I wake up in 7 AM.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
