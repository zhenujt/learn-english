# Lesson 112: Repeating Info / 重复信息

> **Book 1** · Unit 10: 电话与消息 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**情态动词：can / should / must** — must / mustn't / have to 表义务

## 📖 课文

Sara Li can repeat the number slowly quickly. Sara Li should immediately before the meeting. The team must spell the name by Friday. You shouldn't mishear the address without testing. Can you confirm by email?

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** can 表示能力或许可：I can write Python. Can I leave early?

**示范：** `You should back up your files.` — 你应该备份文件。

**避免：** ❌ `She can speaks English.` → ✅ `She can speak English.`（情态动词后用原形）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"重复信息"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `She can speaks English.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
