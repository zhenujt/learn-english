# Lesson 097: Body / 身体

> **Book 1** · Unit 9: 健康与情绪 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**将来时：be going to / will** — be going to 表已有计划

## 📖 课文

Tomorrow, Lin Wei is going to take a break. The release will happen on stretch every hour. We are not going to skip lunch this sprint. Lin Wei will finish the sprint as soon as possible.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** be going to + 动词原形：已计划好的将来行动。I am going to deploy tomorrow.

**示范：** `Don't worry, I will fix it.` — 别担心，我会修复的。

**避免：** ❌ `I will going to help.` → ✅ `I am going to help. / I will help.`（不要混用两个结构）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"身体"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I will going to help.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
