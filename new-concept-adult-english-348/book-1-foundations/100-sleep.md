# Lesson 100: Sleep / 睡眠

> **Book 1** · Unit 9: 健康与情绪 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**将来时：be going to / will** — will 表临时决定、预测和承诺

## 📖 课文

Sara Li is going to sleep earlier next avoid caffeine after 3 PM. The team will work until midnight after the review. Sara Li won't check the phone in bed until the tests pass. "Will you set an alarm?" "Yes, I will."

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** be going to + 动词原形：已计划好的将来行动。I am going to deploy tomorrow.

**示范：** `It is going to rain — take an umbrella.` — 要下雨了——带把伞。

**避免：** ❌ `I will going to help.` → ✅ `I am going to help. / I will help.`（不要混用两个结构）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"睡眠"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I will going to help.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
