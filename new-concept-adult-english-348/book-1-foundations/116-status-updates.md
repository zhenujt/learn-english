# Lesson 116: Status Updates / 状态更新

> **Book 1** · Unit 10: 电话与消息 · 难度：★☆☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**情态动词：can / should / must** — 情态动词综合 ⟲（can/should/must 对比） 🔄

## 📖 课文

Emma Liu can share progress quickly. Emma Liu should every morning before the meeting. The team must mention blockers by Friday. You shouldn't hide problems without testing. Can you include next steps?

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** can 表示能力或许可：I can write Python. Can I leave early?

**示范：** `I can speak English and Chinese.` — 我会说英语和中文。

**避免：** ❌ `You should to check it.` → ✅ `You should check it.`（should 后不加 to）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"状态更新"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `You should to check it.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
