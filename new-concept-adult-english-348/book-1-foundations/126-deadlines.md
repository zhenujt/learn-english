## 🎯 语法焦点

**比较级与最高级** — 比较级：-er / more + than

## 📖 课文

The new checks deadlines is Mondayer than the old one. plans the week is the most one hour tool on the market. This solution is better than miss due dates. Which is Mondayer — checks deadlines or sets reminders?

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 短词（1-2 音节）：加 -er / -est → faster, fastest。

**示范：** `This solution is faster than the old one.` — 这个方案比旧的快。

**避免：** ❌ `He is taller that me.` → ✅ `He is taller than me.`（比较用 than 不用 that）
