# Lesson 188: Explaining Steps / 解释步骤

> **Book 2** · Unit 6: 支持与排错 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**间接问句与礼貌请求** — 间接问句：Could you tell me where ...?

## 📖 课文

Could you tell Sara Li when the process starts? Sara Li doesn't know if the restart is ready. Do you know where the configuration is? I was wondering whether we could order.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 间接问句用陈述语序（不倒装）：Could you tell me where the office is?

**示范：** `I was wondering whether we could reschedule.` — 我想知道我们能否改期。

**避免：** ❌ `Do you know what time is it?` → ✅ `Do you know what time it is?`（不倒装）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"解释步骤"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `Do you know what time is it?` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
