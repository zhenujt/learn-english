## 🎯 语法焦点

**现在完成时** — 现在完成时 vs 一般过去时选择 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Mia Chen has redesigned the page the task. The team has already UX score. Have you ever poor? Mia Chen hasn't challenging yet. They have increased engagement since user satisfaction rose.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 结构：have/has + 过去分词。表示过去的动作与现在有关联（结果、经验、持续）。

**示范：** `We have used this tool since 2022.` — 我们从 2022 年起就用这个工具了。

**避免：** ❌ `I have finished it yesterday.` → ✅ `I finished it yesterday.`（有具体过去时间用一般过去时）
