# Lesson 233: Travel Narrative / 旅行叙述

> **Book 2** · Unit 12: 综合实践 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**定语从句：who / which / that** — 从句 + 连接词综合 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

The developer who booked is Lin Wei. The framework, which is flew, works well. The task that checked in has been completed. Lin Wei is the person who tried.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** who 修饰人：The developer who fixed the bug got a bonus.

**示范：** `The meeting, which lasted two hours, was productive.` — 这个持续两小时的会议很有成果。

**避免：** ❌ `My laptop, that is new, works well.` → ✅ `My laptop, which is new, works well.`（非限定性从句不用 that）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"旅行叙述"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `My laptop, that is new, works well.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
