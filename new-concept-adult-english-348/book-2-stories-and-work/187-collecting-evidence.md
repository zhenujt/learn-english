# Lesson 187: Collecting Evidence / 收集证据

> **Book 2** · Unit 6: 支持与排错 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**间接问句与礼貌请求** — 间接问句：Could you tell me where ...?

## 📖 课文

Tom Park asked, "Do you know what the timestamp is?" "I'm not sure how the affected users works." "Could you explain where the error code goes?" "I was wondering if you could environment."

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 间接问句用陈述语序（不倒装）：Could you tell me where the office is?

**示范：** `Do you know if she is available?` — 你知道她是否有空吗？

**避免：** ❌ `Could you tell me where is the office?` → ✅ `Could you tell me where the office is?`（间接问句用陈述语序）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"收集证据"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `Could you tell me where is the office?` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
