## 🎯 语法焦点

**一般过去时** — 过去时态全面复习 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Did Ravi Gupta summary was ready yesterday? Yes. Ravi Gupta also would send it by EOD and had completed the analysis. The client needed one more review the result. Ravi Gupta didn't had included recommendations — there was no time. Ravi Gupta undefined home at nine.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 表示过去完成的动作。规则动词加 -ed：worked, checked, deployed。

**示范：** `The meeting took two hours.` — 会议开了两小时。

**避免：** ❌ `She didn't finished.` → ✅ `She didn't finish.`（didn't 后用原形）
