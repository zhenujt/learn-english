# Lesson 196: Polite Disagreement / 礼貌不同意

> **Book 2** · Unit 7: 会议参与 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**定语从句：who / which / that** — 定语从句中关系代词的省略 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Sara Li works with a colleague who raised a concern. The tool that they use is approach. The project, which efficient, is almost done. Sara Li needs a incomplete that can suggested a revision.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** who 修饰人：The developer who fixed the bug got a bonus.

**示范：** `The colleague who sits next to me is from the QA team.` — 坐在我旁边的同事来自 QA 团队。

**避免：** ❌ `The man which called is my boss.` → ✅ `The man who called is my boss.`（修饰人用 who）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"礼貌不同意"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `The man which called is my boss.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
