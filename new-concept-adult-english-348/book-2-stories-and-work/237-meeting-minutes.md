# Lesson 237: Meeting Minutes / 会议纪要

> **Book 2** · Unit 12: 综合实践 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**间接引语** — 间接引语 + 被动综合 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

"I will had discussed alternatives tomorrow," Alex Wang said. → Alex Wang said that he/she would had discussed alternatives the next day. "decision was final is would implement next sprint." → Alex Wang said the decision was final was would implement next sprint.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 直接引语变间接引语：时态后退一步 (backshift)。

**示范：** `She told me she would send the report.` — 她告诉我她会发报告。

**避免：** ❌ `He said that "I will go."` → ✅ `He said that he would go.`（间接引语不加引号，时态后退）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"会议纪要"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `He said that "I will go."` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
