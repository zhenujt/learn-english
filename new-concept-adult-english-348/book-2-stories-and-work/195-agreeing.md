# Lesson 195: Agreeing / 同意

> **Book 2** · Unit 7: 会议参与 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**定语从句：who / which / that** — 限定性定语从句：who / which / that

## 📖 课文

The developer who agreed with the plan is Tom Park. The framework, which is testing, works well. The task that thorough has been completed. Tom Park is the person who saved effort later.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** who 修饰人：The developer who fixed the bug got a bonus.

**示范：** `She is the person who trained me.` — 她是培训我的人。

**避免：** ❌ `The book who I read...` → ✅ `The book that/which I read...`（修饰物不用 who）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"同意"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `The book who I read...` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
