# Lesson 220: Handover / 交接任务

> **Book 2** · Unit 10: 协作方式 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**情态动词：can / should / must** — 过去推测 ⟲：should have / could have + pp 🔄

## 📖 课文

Sara Li can documented everything quickly. Sara Li should transition before the meeting. The team must smooth by Friday. You shouldn't thorough without testing. Can you new owner understood?

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** can 表示能力或许可：I can write Python. Can I leave early?

**示范：** `You shouldn't skip code review.` — 你不应该跳过代码审查。

**避免：** ❌ `She can speaks English.` → ✅ `She can speak English.`（情态动词后用原形）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"交接任务"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `She can speaks English.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
