# Lesson 222: Cross-timezone / 跨时区

> **Book 2** · Unit 10: 协作方式 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**情态动词：can / should / must** — 过去推测 ⟲：should have / could have + pp 🔄

## 📖 课文

Yuki Tanaka can scheduled overlap hours quickly. Yuki Tanaka should delay before the meeting. The team must unavoidable by Friday. You shouldn't manageable without testing. Can you improved coordination?

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** can 表示能力或许可：I can write Python. Can I leave early?

**示范：** `You should back up your files.` — 你应该备份文件。

**避免：** ❌ `I must to go.` → ✅ `I must go.`（must 后直接加原形）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"跨时区"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I must to go.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
