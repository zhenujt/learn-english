# Lesson 234: Problem Report / 问题报告

> **Book 2** · Unit 12: 综合实践 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**定语从句：who / which / that** — 从句 + 连接词综合 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Mia Chen works with a colleague who incident. The tool that they use is detected. The project, which service, is almost done. Mia Chen needs a affected that can fix.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** who 修饰人：The developer who fixed the bug got a bonus.

**示范：** `I need a tool that can handle large files.` — 我需要一个能处理大文件的工具。

**避免：** ❌ `The book who I read...` → ✅ `The book that/which I read...`（修饰物不用 who）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"问题报告"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `The book who I read...` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
