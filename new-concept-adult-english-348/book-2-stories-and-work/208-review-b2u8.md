# Lesson 208: Review B2U8 / 复习

> **Book 2** · Unit 8: 产品与用户 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**连接词：because / so / although / however** — 连接词 + 从句综合 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Emma Liu manages the backlog because the experienced was coordinates releases. Although it was mentors new joiners, Emma Liu drives quality. However, the undefined still needs work.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** because 原因 → I stayed late because the release was urgent.

**示范：** `Although the task was hard, she finished it on time.` — 虽然任务很难，她还是按时完成了。

**避免：** ❌ `Because I was tired. I went home.` → ✅ `Because I was tired, I went home.`（because 引导从句，不单独成句）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"复习"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `Because I was tired. I went home.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
