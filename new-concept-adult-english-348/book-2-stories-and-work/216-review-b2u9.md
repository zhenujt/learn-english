# Lesson 216: Review B2U9 / 复习

> **Book 2** · Unit 9: 数据与结果 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**一般过去时** — 过去时态全面复习 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Yesterday, Emma Liu system was stable early. Emma Liu would update the team the new feature and had resolved the issue it to the team. The demo planned to document it well. After work, Emma Liu had informed all stakeholders and undefined.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 表示过去完成的动作。规则动词加 -ed：worked, checked, deployed。

**示范：** `I fixed the bug yesterday.` — 我昨天修了那个缺陷。

**避免：** ❌ `Did he went?` → ✅ `Did he go?`（Did 后用原形）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"复习"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `Did he went?` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
