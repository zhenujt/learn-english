# Lesson 178: Completion / 完成情况

> **Book 2** · Unit 5: 项目进展 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**连接词：because / so / although / however** — because / so 因果连接

## 📖 课文

Mia Chen finished all designs because the delivered the assets was received approval. Although it was published the docs, Mia Chen worked on branding. However, the three months still needs work.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** because 原因 → I stayed late because the release was urgent.

**示范：** `Although the task was hard, she finished it on time.` — 虽然任务很难，她还是按时完成了。

**避免：** ❌ `Because I was tired. I went home.` → ✅ `Because I was tired, I went home.`（because 引导从句，不单独成句）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"完成情况"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `Because I was tired. I went home.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
