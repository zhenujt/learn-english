# Lesson 232: Review B2U11 / 复习

> **Book 2** · Unit 11: 职业成长 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**定语从句：who / which / that** — 从句 + 连接词综合 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Emma Liu works with a colleague who grown professionally. The tool that they use is expanded the skill set. The project, which contributed to open source, is almost done. Emma Liu needs a mentored others that can built a portfolio.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** who 修饰人：The developer who fixed the bug got a bonus.

**示范：** `The framework that we chose is lightweight.` — 我们选的框架很轻量。

**避免：** ❌ `The man which called is my boss.` → ✅ `The man who called is my boss.`（修饰人用 who）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"复习"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `The man which called is my boss.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
