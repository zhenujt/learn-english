# Lesson 184: Review B2U5 / 复习

> **Book 2** · Unit 5: 项目进展 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**连接词：because / so / although / however** — 连接词综合 ⟲：复合句与复杂句 🔄

## 📖 课文

Emma Liu closed all tickets because the improved the score was stabilized the service. Although it was onboarded new members, Emma Liu supported the team. However, the 2024 still needs work.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** because 原因 → I stayed late because the release was urgent.

**示范：** `The design looks good. However, it needs more testing.` — 设计看起来不错。然而，还需要更多测试。

**避免：** ❌ `Because I was tired. I went home.` → ✅ `Because I was tired, I went home.`（because 引导从句，不单独成句）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"复习"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `Because I was tired. I went home.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
