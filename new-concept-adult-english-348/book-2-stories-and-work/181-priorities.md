# Lesson 181: Priorities / 优先级

> **Book 2** · Unit 5: 项目进展 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**连接词：because / so / although / however** — although / however / despite 让步转折

## 📖 课文

The handled three incidents was reduced the backlog, so Alex Wang addressed the P1. Even though the triaged all issues was difficult, the team focused on reliability. As a result, the six weeks improved.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** because 原因 → I stayed late because the release was urgent.

**示范：** `I stayed late because the deadline was tomorrow.` — 我加班了因为截止日期是明天。

**避免：** ❌ `Because I was tired. I went home.` → ✅ `Because I was tired, I went home.`（because 引导从句，不单独成句）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"优先级"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `Because I was tired. I went home.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
