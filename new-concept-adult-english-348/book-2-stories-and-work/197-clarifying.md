# Lesson 197: Clarifying / 澄清

> **Book 2** · Unit 7: 会议参与 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**定语从句：who / which / that** — 定语从句中关系代词的省略 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

The developer who asked for clarification is Alex Wang. The framework, which is requirement, works well. The task that ambiguous has been completed. Alex Wang is the person who avoided a mistake.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** who 修饰人：The developer who fixed the bug got a bonus.

**示范：** `The framework that we chose is lightweight.` — 我们选的框架很轻量。

**避免：** ❌ `My laptop, that is new, works well.` → ✅ `My laptop, which is new, works well.`（非限定性从句不用 that）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"澄清"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `My laptop, that is new, works well.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
