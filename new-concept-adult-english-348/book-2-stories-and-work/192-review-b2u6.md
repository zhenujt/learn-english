# Lesson 192: Review B2U6 / 复习

> **Book 2** · Unit 6: 支持与排错 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**间接问句与礼貌请求** — 礼貌请求 ⟲：I was wondering if ... 🔄

## 📖 课文

Could you tell Emma Liu when the meeting time starts? Emma Liu doesn't know if the agenda is ready. Do you know where the availability is? I was wondering whether we could format.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 间接问句用陈述语序（不倒装）：Could you tell me where the office is?

**示范：** `Do you know if she is available?` — 你知道她是否有空吗？

**避免：** ❌ `I wonder that she is ready.` → ✅ `I wonder if/whether she is ready.`（是否用 if/whether）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"复习"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I wonder that she is ready.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
