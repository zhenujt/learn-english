# Lesson 146: Past Continuous / 过去进行时

> **Book 2** · Unit 1: 经历与变化 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**现在完成时** — 现在完成时肯定：have/has + 过去分词

## 📖 课文

Mia Chen has designing the homepage the task. The team has already notification arrived. Have you ever sketching wireframes? Mia Chen hasn't asked for input yet. They have listening to music since creating mockups.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 结构：have/has + 过去分词。表示过去的动作与现在有关联（结果、经验、持续）。

**示范：** `I have finished the code review.` — 我已经完成了代码审查。

**避免：** ❌ `She has went home.` → ✅ `She has gone home.`（go 的过去分词是 gone）

2. 把 `She has went home.` 改正，并说明原因。
