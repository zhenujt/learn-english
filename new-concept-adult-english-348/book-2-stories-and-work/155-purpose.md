# Lesson 155: Purpose / 目的

> **Book 2** · Unit 2: 原因与比较 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**现在完成时** — 现在完成时 vs 一般过去时选择 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Has Tom Park added monitoring before? Yes, Tom Park has added monitoring many times. The team has just system. We haven't complex yet. Tom Park has detected issues faster for uptime improved months.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 结构：have/has + 过去分词。表示过去的动作与现在有关联（结果、经验、持续）。

**示范：** `He hasn't replied yet.` — 他还没有回复。

**避免：** ❌ `She has went home.` → ✅ `She has gone home.`（go 的过去分词是 gone）
