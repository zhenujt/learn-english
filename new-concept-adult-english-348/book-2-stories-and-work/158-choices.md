# Lesson 158: Choices / 选择

> **Book 2** · Unit 2: 原因与比较 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**比较级与最高级** — 比较级深化：less ... / as ... as / not as ... as

## 📖 课文

The new option is cost-effectiveer than the old one. plan A is the most flexible tool on the market. This solution is better than plan B. Which is cost-effectiveer — option or risky?

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 短词（1-2 音节）：加 -er / -est → faster, fastest。

**示范：** `She is the most experienced developer on the team.` — 她是团队中最有经验的开发者。

**避免：** ❌ `This is the most fastest.` → ✅ `This is the fastest.`（最高级不能加 most）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"选择"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `This is the most fastest.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
