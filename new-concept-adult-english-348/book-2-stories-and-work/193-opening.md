# Lesson 193: Opening / 开场

> **Book 2** · Unit 7: 会议参与 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**定语从句：who / which / that** — 限定性定语从句：who / which / that

## 📖 课文

The developer who started the meeting is Lin Wei. The framework, which is agenda, works well. The task that clear has been completed. Lin Wei is the person who covered all points.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** who 修饰人：The developer who fixed the bug got a bonus.

**示范：** `The meeting, which lasted two hours, was productive.` — 这个持续两小时的会议很有成果。

**避免：** ❌ `The man which called is my boss.` → ✅ `The man who called is my boss.`（修饰人用 who）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"开场"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `The man which called is my boss.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
