# Lesson 240: Book 2 Graduation / 毕业复习

> **Book 2** · Unit 12: 综合实践 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**连接词：because / so / although / however** — 第二册毕业综合 ⟲（全部中级结构） 🔄

## 📖 课文

Emma Liu reflected on the year because the growth was significant. Although it was challenging, Emma Liu set new objectives. However, the confidence increased still needs work.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** because 原因 → I stayed late because the release was urgent.

**示范：** `Even though I was tired, I reviewed the PR.` — 尽管我很累，我还是审查了 PR。

**避免：** ❌ `I was tired, however I continued.` → ✅ `I was tired; however, I continued.`（however 前用句号或分号）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"毕业复习"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I was tired, however I continued.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
