# Lesson 194: Giving Opinions / 表达意见

> **Book 2** · Unit 7: 会议参与 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**定语从句：who / which / that** — 限定性定语从句：who / which / that

## 📖 课文

Mia Chen works with a colleague who shared her view. The tool that they use is design. The project, which valid, is almost done. Mia Chen needs a risky that can persuaded the team.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** who 修饰人：The developer who fixed the bug got a bonus.

**示范：** `I need a tool that can handle large files.` — 我需要一个能处理大文件的工具。

**避免：** ❌ `My laptop, that is new, works well.` → ✅ `My laptop, which is new, works well.`（非限定性从句不用 that）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"表达意见"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `My laptop, that is new, works well.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
