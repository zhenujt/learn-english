# Lesson 150: Life Experiences / 人生经历

> **Book 2** · Unit 1: 经历与变化 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**现在完成时** — already / yet / just 与完成时

## 📖 课文

Yuki Tanaka has traveled to five countries the task. The team has already presented at a conference. Have you ever led a cross-functional team? Yuki Tanaka hasn't mentored a junior dev yet. They have contributed to open source since handled a production incident.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 结构：have/has + 过去分词。表示过去的动作与现在有关联（结果、经验、持续）。

**示范：** `He hasn't replied yet.` — 他还没有回复。

**避免：** ❌ `Did you ever been to Japan?` → ✅ `Have you ever been to Japan?`（经验用现在完成时）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"人生经历"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `Did you ever been to Japan?` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
