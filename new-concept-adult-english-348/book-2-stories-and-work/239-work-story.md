# Lesson 239: Work Story / 工作故事

> **Book 2** · Unit 12: 综合实践 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**连接词：because / so / although / however** — 第二册毕业综合 ⟲（全部中级结构） 🔄

## 📖 课文

The fire alarm went off was preparing the demo, so Ravi Gupta presenting the quarterly results. Even though the client called unexpectedly was difficult, the team running the final test. As a result, the debugging late improved.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** because 原因 → I stayed late because the release was urgent.

**示范：** `The design looks good. However, it needs more testing.` — 设计看起来不错。然而，还需要更多测试。

**避免：** ❌ `Although it rained, but I went out.` → ✅ `Although it rained, I went out.`（although 和 but 不能同时用）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"工作故事"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `Although it rained, but I went out.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
