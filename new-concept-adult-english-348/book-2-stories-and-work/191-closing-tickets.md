# Lesson 191: Closing Tickets / 关闭工单

> **Book 2** · Unit 6: 支持与排错 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**间接问句与礼貌请求** — 礼貌请求 ⟲：I was wondering if ... 🔄

## 📖 课文

Ravi Gupta asked, "Do you know what the resolution is?" "I'm not sure how the verification works." "Could you explain where the customer confirmation goes?" "I was wondering if you could documentation."

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 间接问句用陈述语序（不倒装）：Could you tell me where the office is?

**示范：** `Could you tell me when the meeting starts?` — 你能告诉我会议什么时候开始吗？

**避免：** ❌ `Do you know what time is it?` → ✅ `Do you know what time it is?`（不倒装）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"关闭工单"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `Do you know what time is it?` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
