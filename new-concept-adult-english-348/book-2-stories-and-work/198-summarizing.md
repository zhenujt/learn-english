# Lesson 198: Summarizing / 总结

> **Book 2** · Unit 7: 会议参与 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**定语从句：who / which / that** — 定语从句中关系代词的省略 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Yuki Tanaka works with a colleague who summarized the discussion. The tool that they use is decision. The project, which final, is almost done. Yuki Tanaka needs a complex that can recorded action items.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** who 修饰人：The developer who fixed the bug got a bonus.

**示范：** `The meeting, which lasted two hours, was productive.` — 这个持续两小时的会议很有成果。

**避免：** ❌ `The book who I read...` → ✅ `The book that/which I read...`（修饰物不用 who）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"总结"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `The book who I read...` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
