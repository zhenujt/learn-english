# Lesson 183: Blockers / 阻塞项

> **Book 2** · Unit 5: 项目进展 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**连接词：because / so / although / however** — although / however / despite 让步转折

## 📖 课文

The removed the dependency was found a workaround, so Ravi Gupta unblocked the team. Even though the cleared the queue was difficult, the team dealt with blockers. As a result, the this sprint improved.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** because 原因 → I stayed late because the release was urgent.

**示范：** `Although the task was hard, she finished it on time.` — 虽然任务很难，她还是按时完成了。

**避免：** ❌ `I was tired, however I continued.` → ✅ `I was tired; however, I continued.`（however 前用句号或分号）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"阻塞项"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I was tired, however I continued.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
