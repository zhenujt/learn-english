## 🎯 语法焦点

**间接引语** — 间接引语 + 被动综合 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Sara Li said that the completed the sprint was delivered four features. She told the team she would resolved all blockers. He asked if we could updated documentation. They explained that the worked overtime had this week.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 直接引语变间接引语：时态后退一步 (backshift)。

**示范：** `He said that he was working on the fix.` — 他说他正在修复中。

**避免：** ❌ `She said me that...` → ✅ `She told me that...`（say 不加人，tell 加人）

2. 把 `She said me that...` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
