# Lesson 189: Temporary Solutions / 临时方案

> **Book 2** · Unit 6: 支持与排错 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**间接问句与礼貌请求** — 间接问句：Could you tell me where ...?

## 📖 课文

Alex Wang asked, "Do you know what the workaround is?" "I'm not sure how the risk works." "Could you explain where the duration goes?" "I was wondering if you could approval."

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 间接问句用陈述语序（不倒装）：Could you tell me where the office is?

**示范：** `Can you explain how this works?` — 你能解释一下这是怎么运作的吗？

**避免：** ❌ `I wonder that she is ready.` → ✅ `I wonder if/whether she is ready.`（是否用 if/whether）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"临时方案"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I wonder that she is ready.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
