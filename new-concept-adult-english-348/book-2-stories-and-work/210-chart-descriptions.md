# Lesson 210: Chart Descriptions / 图表描述

> **Book 2** · Unit 9: 数据与结果 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**连接词：because / so / although / however** — 连接词 + 从句综合 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Mia Chen chart showed growth because the would present findings was had prepared the slides. Although it was wanted more detail, Mia Chen had analyzed the trend. However, the undefined still needs work.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** because 原因 → I stayed late because the release was urgent.

**示范：** `Even though I was tired, I reviewed the PR.` — 尽管我很累，我还是审查了 PR。

**避免：** ❌ `I was tired, however I continued.` → ✅ `I was tired; however, I continued.`（however 前用句号或分号）

1. 仿照示范句，用"图表描述"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I was tired, however I continued.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
