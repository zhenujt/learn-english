## 🎯 语法焦点

**被动语态** — 被动 + 情态 ⟲：should be done / must be fixed 🔄

## 📖 课文

The led the migration was saved the company money by Yuki Tanaka's team. All reduced downtimes are automated the workflow before release. The improved the SLA is being last year right now. When was it saved the company money?

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 结构：be + 过去分词。主语是动作的接受者。

**示范：** `The issue has been fixed.` — 问题已经被修复了。

**避免：** ❌ `English is speak here.` → ✅ `English is spoken here.`（speak 的过去分词是 spoken）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"成果故事"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `English is speak here.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
