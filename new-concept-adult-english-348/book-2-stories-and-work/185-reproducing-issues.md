# Lesson 185: Reproducing Issues / 复现问题

> **Book 2** · Unit 6: 支持与排错 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**连接词：because / so / although / however** — 连接词综合 ⟲：复合句与复杂句 🔄

## 📖 课文

The steps was log file, so Lin Wei error occurs. Even though the version was difficult, the team reproduce it. As a result, the expected behavior improved.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** because 原因 → I stayed late because the release was urgent.

**示范：** `Even though I was tired, I reviewed the PR.` — 尽管我很累，我还是审查了 PR。

**避免：** ❌ `Although it rained, but I went out.` → ✅ `Although it rained, I went out.`（although 和 but 不能同时用）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"复现问题"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `Although it rained, but I went out.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
