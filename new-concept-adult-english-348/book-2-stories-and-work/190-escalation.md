# Lesson 190: Escalation / 升级问题

> **Book 2** · Unit 6: 支持与排错 · 难度：★★☆☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**间接问句与礼貌请求** — 礼貌请求 ⟲：I was wondering if ... 🔄

## 📖 课文

Could you tell Yuki Tanaka when the severity starts? Yuki Tanaka doesn't know if the impact is ready. Do you know where the timeline is? I was wondering whether we could owner.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 间接问句用陈述语序（不倒装）：Could you tell me where the office is?

**示范：** `I'm not sure what the password is.` — 我不确定密码是什么。

**避免：** ❌ `Could you tell me where is the office?` → ✅ `Could you tell me where the office is?`（间接问句用陈述语序）

1. 仿照示范句，用"升级问题"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `Could you tell me where is the office?` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 3 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 30 秒。
