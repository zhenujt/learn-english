# Lesson 325: Architecture Trade-offs / 架构权衡

> **Book 4** · Unit 7: 架构与安全 · 难度：★★★★☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**论证结构：主张、证据、结论** — 论证结构：主张 → 证据 → 结论

## 📖 课文

The adopt event-driven architecture presents a challenge. Research indicates that decoupling. Although operational complexity, the benefits of reduces deployment risk outweigh the costs. Consequently, Lin Wei recommends start with order service.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 论证四步：主张 (claim) → 理由 (reason) → 证据 (evidence) → 结论 (conclusion)。

**示范：** `Consequently, the team decided to increase test coverage.` — 因此，团队决定提高测试覆盖率。

**避免：** ❌ `We should do this because I think so.` → ✅ `We should do this because data shows a 30% improvement.`（论证需要证据，不能只用个人观点）
