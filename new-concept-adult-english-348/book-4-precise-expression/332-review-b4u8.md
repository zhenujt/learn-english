# Lesson 332: Review B4U8 / 复习

> **Book 4** · Unit 8: 数据驱动建议 · 难度：★★★★☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**论证结构：主张、证据、结论** — 论证综合 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Sara Li proposes that we data summary. The primary reason is methodology. Evidence shows that findings. Admittedly, limitations; however, recommendations. Therefore, we recommend next steps.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 论证四步：主张 (claim) → 理由 (reason) → 证据 (evidence) → 结论 (conclusion)。

**示范：** `Admittedly, the cost is higher; however, the long-term savings justify the investment.` — 诚然成本更高；然而长期节省证明了投资的合理性。

**避免：** ❌ `The cost is high but we do it anyway.` → ✅ `Although the cost is significant, the long-term benefit outweighs it.`（需要明确权衡）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"复习"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `The cost is high but we do it anyway.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
