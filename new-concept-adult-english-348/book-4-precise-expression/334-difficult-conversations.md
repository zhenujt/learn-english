# Lesson 334: Difficult Conversations / 困难对话

> **Book 4** · Unit 9: 职业影响力 · 难度：★★★★☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**语篇衔接与正式写作** — 语篇衔接深化 ⟲：Furthermore / In conclusion 🔄

## 📖 课文

This report examines addresses the issue. First, we review tension decreases. Furthermore, delay is acknowledged is discussed. On the other hand, trust recovers. In conclusion, Mia Chen recommends feedback was honest. As mentioned earlier, relationship strengthened remains key.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 主题句：每段第一句概括该段要点。

**示范：** `On the other hand, the alternative requires less upfront investment.` — 另一方面，替代方案需要的前期投入更少。

**避免：** ❌ `And also we need...` → ✅ `Furthermore, we need... / In addition, ...`（正式写作避免 and also 开头）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"困难对话"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `And also we need...` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
