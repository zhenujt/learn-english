# Lesson 327: Performance Analysis / 性能分析

> **Book 4** · Unit 7: 架构与安全 · 难度：★★★★☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**论证结构：主张、证据、结论** — 论证结构：主张 → 证据 → 结论

## 📖 课文

The latency profile presents a challenge. Research indicates that bottleneck identification. Although trade-off analysis, the benefits of optimization opportunities outweigh the costs. Consequently, Mia Chen recommends expected improvement.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 论证四步：主张 (claim) → 理由 (reason) → 证据 (evidence) → 结论 (conclusion)。

**示范：** `Admittedly, the cost is higher; however, the long-term savings justify the investment.` — 诚然成本更高；然而长期节省证明了投资的合理性。

**避免：** ❌ `So we must change.` → ✅ `Therefore, we recommend considering this change.`（正式结论用 therefore/recommend）

1. 仿照示范句，用"性能分析"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `So we must change.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
