## 📖 课文

This report examines implemented circuit breakers. First, we review contained the blast radius. Furthermore, cascade is discussed. On the other hand, been prevented. In conclusion, Mia Chen recommends added health checks. As mentioned earlier, detected the issue earlier remains key.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 主题句：每段第一句概括该段要点。

**示范：** `Furthermore, the new system offers better monitoring capabilities.` — 此外，新系统提供了更好的监控能力。

**避免：** ❌ `And also we need...` → ✅ `Furthermore, we need... / In addition, ...`（正式写作避免 and also 开头）
