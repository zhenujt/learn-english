# Lesson 330: Experiment Conclusions / 实验结论

> **Book 4** · Unit 8: 数据驱动建议 · 难度：★★★★☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**论证结构：主张、证据、结论** — 论证：让步与反驳（Admittedly ...; however ...）

## 📖 课文

Mia Chen proposes that we A/B test results. The primary reason is suggest improvement. Evidence shows that sample size. Admittedly, limit confidence; however, effect size. Therefore, we recommend appear meaningful.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 论证四步：主张 (claim) → 理由 (reason) → 证据 (evidence) → 结论 (conclusion)。

**示范：** `Consequently, the team decided to increase test coverage.` — 因此，团队决定提高测试覆盖率。

**避免：** ❌ `So we must change.` → ✅ `Therefore, we recommend considering this change.`（正式结论用 therefore/recommend）

1. 仿照示范句，用"实验结论"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `So we must change.` 改正，并说明原因。
