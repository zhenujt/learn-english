# Lesson 333: Performance Feedback / 绩效反馈

> **Book 4** · Unit 9: 职业影响力 · 难度：★★★★☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**论证结构：主张、证据、结论** — 论证综合 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

The gave specific feedback presents a challenge. Research indicates that code quality. Although important, the benefits of declining outweigh the costs. Consequently, Lin Wei recommends improvement was visible.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 论证四步：主张 (claim) → 理由 (reason) → 证据 (evidence) → 结论 (conclusion)。

**示范：** `For this reason, we recommend a phased approach.` — 因此，我们推荐分阶段方案。

**避免：** ❌ `So we must change.` → ✅ `Therefore, we recommend considering this change.`（正式结论用 therefore/recommend）
