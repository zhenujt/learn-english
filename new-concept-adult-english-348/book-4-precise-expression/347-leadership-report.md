## 📖 课文

The following section addresses engineering health. In addition to delivery velocity, we consider quality metrics. However, team engagement should not be overlooked. To summarize, the best approach is technical debt.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 主题句：每段第一句概括该段要点。

**示范：** `In conclusion, we recommend proceeding with option A.` — 总之，我们建议选择方案 A。

**避免：** ❌ `Like I said before...` → ✅ `As mentioned earlier, ...`（正式引用前文用 as mentioned）
