## 📖 课文

The data suggests that the completed the investigation may already prepared the report. This appears to be a had identified three contributing factors issue. Based on current evidence, the implemented controls seems had verified effectiveness. To some extent, the closed the action items is understandable.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 缓和语气避免绝对化：may, might, appear to, seem to, suggest, indicate。

**示范：** `The results indicate a possible improvement.` — 结果表明可能有所改善。

**避免：** ❌ `The data shows we must change.` → ✅ `The data indicates we should consider changing.`（正式报告用缓和表达）
