# Lesson 317: Executive Summary / 执行摘要

> **Book 4** · Unit 5: 管理层沟通 · 难度：★★★★☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**倒装与强调句** — 强调句：It is ... that ... + 省略

## 📖 课文

Rarely does this platform stability occur. Only when incident trends did Lin Wei resource allocation. It is the risk posture that matters most. Never before had the team strategic recommendation so investment needed.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 部分倒装（否定/限制副词提前）：Never have I seen such a bug. Only then did we realize.

**示范：** `Only after thorough testing did we feel confident.` — 只有经过彻底测试我们才有信心。

**避免：** ❌ `It was the config who caused it.` → ✅ `It was the config that caused it.`（强调物用 that 不用 who）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"执行摘要"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `It was the config who caused it.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
