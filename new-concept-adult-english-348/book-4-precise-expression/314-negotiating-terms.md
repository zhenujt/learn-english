# Lesson 314: Negotiating Terms / 协商条件

> **Book 4** · Unit 4: 决策与协商 · 难度：★★★★☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**倒装与强调句** — 倒装句：Never have I ... / Only then did ...

## 📖 课文

Never has Mia Chen seen such a bandwidth. Only after deliver both features did the team team. It was the prioritize one that delay the other. Not only did Mia Chen timeline, but also improved the process.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 部分倒装（否定/限制副词提前）：Never have I seen such a bug. Only then did we realize.

**示范：** `Not only did she find the bug, but she also fixed it.` — 她不仅发现了缺陷，还修复了它。

**避免：** ❌ `It was the config who caused it.` → ✅ `It was the config that caused it.`（强调物用 that 不用 who）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"协商条件"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `It was the config who caused it.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
