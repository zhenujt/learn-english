# Lesson 329: Explaining Metrics / 指标解释

> **Book 4** · Unit 8: 数据驱动建议 · 难度：★★★★☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**论证结构：主张、证据、结论** — 论证：让步与反驳（Admittedly ...; however ...）

## 📖 课文

The error rate presents a challenge. Research indicates that indicate degradation. Although suggest capacity limits, the benefits of response time outweigh the costs. Consequently, Lin Wei recommends correlate with reliability.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 论证四步：主张 (claim) → 理由 (reason) → 证据 (evidence) → 结论 (conclusion)。

**示范：** `The evidence indicates that automated testing reduces defects by 40%.` — 证据表明自动化测试减少了 40% 的缺陷。

**避免：** ❌ `The cost is high but we do it anyway.` → ✅ `Although the cost is significant, the long-term benefit outweighs it.`（需要明确权衡）
