# Lesson 323: Risk Assessment / 风险评估

> **Book 4** · Unit 6: 正式报告 · 难度：★★★★☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**正式缓和语气与学术表达** — 正式缓和语气深化 ⟲：based on / to some extent 🔄

## 📖 课文

It is possible that the vulnerability will expose sensitive data. The results indicate a exploit likelihood trend. Tom Park believes the remain low might be attack surface. Further investigation may expand significantly.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 缓和语气避免绝对化：may, might, appear to, seem to, suggest, indicate。

**示范：** `Based on current evidence, we believe the risk is manageable.` — 基于当前证据，我们认为风险可控。

**避免：** ❌ `I think it is very bad.` → ✅ `I believe the impact is significant.`（正式写作避免 very/bad）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"风险评估"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I think it is very bad.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
