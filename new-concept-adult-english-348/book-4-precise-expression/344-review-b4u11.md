# Lesson 344: Review B4U11 / 复习

> **Book 4** · Unit 11: 公开表达 · 难度：★★★★☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**论证结构：主张、证据、结论** — 全册应用综合 ⟲（论证 + 缓和 + 衔接） 🔄

## 📖 课文

Sara Li proposes that we thesis statement. The primary reason is supporting arguments. Evidence shows that counterargument. Admittedly, rebuttal; however, synthesis. Therefore, we recommend final recommendation.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 论证四步：主张 (claim) → 理由 (reason) → 证据 (evidence) → 结论 (conclusion)。

**示范：** `The evidence indicates that automated testing reduces defects by 40%.` — 证据表明自动化测试减少了 40% 的缺陷。

**避免：** ❌ `The cost is high but we do it anyway.` → ✅ `Although the cost is significant, the long-term benefit outweighs it.`（需要明确权衡）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"复习"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `The cost is high but we do it anyway.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
