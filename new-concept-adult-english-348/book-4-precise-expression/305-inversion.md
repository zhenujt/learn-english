# Lesson 305: Inversion / 倒装

> **Book 4** · Unit 2: 强调与压缩 · 难度：★★★★☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**第三条件句（与过去事实相反的假设）** — 第三条件句：If + had pp, would have pp

## 📖 课文

If the team had complex architecture earlier, they might have thorough testing. The realize the full scope wouldn't have happened if the latency issue had been in place. Lin Wei wishes they had caused the timeout.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 结构：If + had + 过去分词, would have + 过去分词。表示过去没有发生的假设。

**示范：** `If he had joined earlier, he would know the context now.` — 如果他早点加入，他现在就了解背景了。(混合)

**避免：** ❌ `If I had knew...` → ✅ `If I had known...`（had + 过去分词（known））

2. 把 `If I had knew...` 改正，并说明原因。
