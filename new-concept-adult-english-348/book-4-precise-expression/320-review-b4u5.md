# Lesson 320: Review B4U5 / 复习

> **Book 4** · Unit 5: 管理层沟通 · 难度：★★★★☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**倒装与强调句** — 强调与压缩综合 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Never has Sara Li seen such a quarterly business review. Only after revenue impact did the team operational efficiency. It was the customer satisfaction that strategic initiatives. Not only did Sara Li resource request, but also improved the process.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 部分倒装（否定/限制副词提前）：Never have I seen such a bug. Only then did we realize.

**示范：** `Rarely does this error occur in production.` — 这个错误在生产环境中很少出现。

**避免：** ❌ `It was the config who caused it.` → ✅ `It was the config that caused it.`（强调物用 that 不用 who）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"复习"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `It was the config who caused it.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
