# Lesson 348: Book 4 Graduation / 毕业复习

> **Book 4** · Unit 12: 综合实践 · 难度：★★★★☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**语篇衔接与正式写作** — 第四册毕业综合 ⟲（全部高级结构） 🔄

## 📖 课文

This report examines continue professional development. First, we review career growth. Furthermore, evidence of consistent improvement is discussed. On the other hand, time investment. In conclusion, Sara Li recommends compounding returns. As mentioned earlier, commit to weekly practice remains key.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 主题句：每段第一句概括该段要点。

**示范：** `This approach has two main benefits. First, it reduces cost. Second, it improves reliability.` — 这个方案有两个主要好处。第一，降低成本。第二，提高可靠性。

**避免：** ❌ `So basically...` → ✅ `In summary, ... / To conclude, ...`（正式总结不用 so basically）

2. 把 `So basically...` 改正，并说明原因。
