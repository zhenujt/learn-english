# Lesson 309: Claims and Evidence / 主张与证据

> **Book 4** · Unit 3: 正式论证 · 难度：★★★★☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**第三条件句（与过去事实相反的假设）** — 混合条件句 ⟲ + 条件句综合 🔄

## 📖 课文

If the team had migrate to Kubernetes earlier, they might have container orchestration. The deployment frequency increased 3x wouldn't have happened if the learning curve had been in place. Lin Wei wishes they had operational benefits outweigh costs.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 结构：If + had + 过去分词, would have + 过去分词。表示过去没有发生的假设。

**示范：** `The outage could have been avoided if the config had been reviewed.` — 如果配置被审查过，这次宕机本可避免。

**避免：** ❌ `If she studied harder, she would have passed.` → ✅ `If she had studied harder, she would have passed.`（第三条件句 if 从句用 had + pp）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"主张与证据"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `If she studied harder, she would have passed.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
