# Lesson 340: Review B4U10 / 复习

> **Book 4** · Unit 10: 求职与发展 · 难度：★★★★☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**语篇衔接与正式写作** — 正式写作综合 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

This report examines built distributed systems. First, we review managed cross-functional teams. Furthermore, driven technical strategy is discussed. On the other hand, reduced operational costs. In conclusion, Sara Li recommends improved developer experience. As mentioned earlier, seven years remains key.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 主题句：每段第一句概括该段要点。

**示范：** `As mentioned earlier, the primary concern is data security.` — 如前所述，首要关切是数据安全。

**避免：** ❌ `And also we need...` → ✅ `Furthermore, we need... / In addition, ...`（正式写作避免 and also 开头）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"复习"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `And also we need...` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
