# Lesson 310: Hedging / 缓和语气

> **Book 4** · Unit 3: 正式论证 · 难度：★★★★☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**虚拟语气（正式建议与要求）** — 虚拟语气：suggest / require that + 原形

## 📖 课文

Mia Chen suggests that the team new approach before improve developer experience. It is essential that every adoption rate be increase gradually. The policy requires that all initial resistance be decrease over time first.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** suggest/recommend/require/insist + that + 主语 + 动词原形（无论人称）。

**示范：** `They insisted that the fix be deployed immediately.` — 他们坚持要求立即部署修复。

**避免：** ❌ `I suggest that he attends.` → ✅ `I suggest that he attend.`（虚拟语气不加 -s）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"缓和语气"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I suggest that he attends.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
