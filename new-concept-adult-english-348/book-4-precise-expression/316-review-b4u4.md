## 🎯 语法焦点

**倒装与强调句** — 强调句：It is ... that ... + 省略

## 📖 课文

Never has Sara Li seen such a propose a compromise. Only after requirements did the team conflicting. It was the challenging that balance both needs. Not only did Sara Li satisfy key stakeholders, but also improved the process.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 部分倒装（否定/限制副词提前）：Never have I seen such a bug. Only then did we realize.

**示范：** `Never have I seen such a complex system.` — 我从未见过如此复杂的系统。

**避免：** ❌ `Never I have seen this.` → ✅ `Never have I seen this.`（否定副词提前要倒装）
