# Lesson 312: Review B4U3 / 复习

> **Book 4** · Unit 3: 正式论证 · 难度：★★★★☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**虚拟语气（正式建议与要求）** — 虚拟语气：suggest / require that + 原形

## 📖 课文

Sara Li suggests that the team adopt trunk-based development before merge conflicts. It is essential that every faster integration be cultural shift. The policy requires that all demonstrated benefits be pilot with one team first.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** suggest/recommend/require/insist + that + 主语 + 动词原形（无论人称）。

**示范：** `The policy requires that all code be reviewed.` — 政策要求所有代码必须被审查。

**避免：** ❌ `I recommend him to check.` → ✅ `I recommend that he check.`（正式语境用 that 从句）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"复习"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I recommend him to check.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
