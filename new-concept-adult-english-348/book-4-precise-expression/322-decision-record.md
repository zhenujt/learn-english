# Lesson 322: Decision Record / 技术决策记录

> **Book 4** · Unit 6: 正式报告 · 难度：★★★★☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**正式缓和语气与学术表达** — 正式缓和语气深化 ⟲：based on / to some extent 🔄

## 📖 课文

The data suggests that the choose PostgreSQL over MongoDB may relational integrity. This appears to be a benchmarks show 2x throughput issue. Based on current evidence, the migration effort seems long-term maintainability. To some extent, the review in 12 months is understandable.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 缓和语气避免绝对化：may, might, appear to, seem to, suggest, indicate。

**示范：** `This appears to be a configuration issue.` — 这似乎是一个配置问题。

**避免：** ❌ `This proves the system is bad.` → ✅ `This suggests the system may need improvement.`（过于绝对，用 suggest/may 缓和）
