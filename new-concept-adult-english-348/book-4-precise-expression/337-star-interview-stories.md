# Lesson 337: STAR Interview Stories / STAR 面试故事

> **Book 4** · Unit 10: 求职与发展 · 难度：★★★★☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**语篇衔接与正式写作** — 长文结构 ⟲：Introduction → Body → Conclusion 🔄

## 📖 课文

The following section addresses faced a production outage. In addition to already coordinated the response, we consider had identified the root cause. However, implemented the fix should not be overlooked. To summarize, the best approach is had restored service in 30 minutes.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 主题句：每段第一句概括该段要点。

**示范：** `In conclusion, we recommend proceeding with option A.` — 总之，我们建议选择方案 A。

**避免：** ❌ `And also we need...` → ✅ `Furthermore, we need... / In addition, ...`（正式写作避免 and also 开头）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"STAR 面试故事"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `And also we need...` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
