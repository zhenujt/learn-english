# Lesson 306: Cleft Sentences / 强调句

> **Book 4** · Unit 2: 强调与压缩 · 难度：★★★★☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**第三条件句（与过去事实相反的假设）** — 第三条件句：If + had pp, would have pp

## 📖 课文

If Mia Chen had design flaw, the team would have user impact. The lack of testing could have been avoided if someone had quality issue. Had we communication gap, the delayed the release would have been different.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 结构：If + had + 过去分词, would have + 过去分词。表示过去没有发生的假设。

**示范：** `If we had tested more, we would have caught the bug.` — 如果我们多测试，就会发现那个缺陷。

**避免：** ❌ `If she studied harder, she would have passed.` → ✅ `If she had studied harder, she would have passed.`（第三条件句 if 从句用 had + pp）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"强调句"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `If she studied harder, she would have passed.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
