> **Book 4** · Unit 3: 正式论证 · 难度：★★★★☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**虚拟语气（正式建议与要求）** — 虚拟语气：suggest / require that + 原形

## 📖 课文

The manager recommended that Tom Park invest in AI testing the coverage gaps. It is important that the reduces manual effort by 60% be false positives. They insisted that the net time savings be recommend a trial period immediately.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** suggest/recommend/require/insist + that + 主语 + 动词原形（无论人称）。

**示范：** `I suggest that he attend the meeting.` — 我建议他参加这个会议。

**避免：** ❌ `It is important that she goes.` → ✅ `It is important that she go.`（无论人称一律用原形）
