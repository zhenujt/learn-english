# Lesson 338: Professional Profile / 职业简介

> **Book 4** · Unit 10: 求职与发展 · 难度：★★★★☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**语篇衔接与正式写作** — 长文结构 ⟲：Introduction → Body → Conclusion 🔄

## 📖 课文

This report examines led design systems. First, we review delivered enterprise products. Furthermore, mentored five designers is discussed. On the other hand, improved accessibility scores. In conclusion, Mia Chen recommends built component libraries. As mentioned earlier, since 2019 remains key.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 主题句：每段第一句概括该段要点。

**示范：** `This approach has two main benefits. First, it reduces cost. Second, it improves reliability.` — 这个方案有两个主要好处。第一，降低成本。第二，提高可靠性。

**避免：** ❌ `Like I said before...` → ✅ `As mentioned earlier, ...`（正式引用前文用 as mentioned）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"职业简介"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `Like I said before...` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
