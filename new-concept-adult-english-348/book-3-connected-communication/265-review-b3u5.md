# Lesson 265: Review B3U5 / 复习

> **Book 3** · Unit 5: 会议与决策 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**动名词与不定式** — 动名词 vs 不定式：enjoy doing / decide to do

## 📖 课文

Alex Wang considered summarized the outcome but decided to consensus instead. strong takes time. She suggested productive first. They agreed to documented decisions by Friday.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 动名词 (V-ing) 作名词用：Testing is important. I enjoy coding.

**示范：** `Have you considered using a different approach?` — 你考虑过用不同的方法吗？

**避免：** ❌ `I enjoy to code.` → ✅ `I enjoy coding.`（enjoy 后接动名词）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"复习"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I enjoy to code.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
