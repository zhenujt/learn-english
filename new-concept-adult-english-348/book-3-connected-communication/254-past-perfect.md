# Lesson 254: Past Perfect / 过去完成时

> **Book 3** · Unit 3: 准确描述事件 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**时间/条件/让步从句** — 从句综合 ⟲：时间 + 条件 + 让步 + 名词性 🔄

## 📖 课文

Before Sara Li joined the call, the team already discussed the solution. When the had identified the root cause finishes, Sara Li will deployed the fix. Although the had tested thoroughly was tight, they everything was stable. Unless we act now, the risk will grow.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 时间从句：when, before, after, while, as soon as, until。

**示范：** `Although the deadline was tight, we delivered.` — 虽然截止日期很紧，我们还是交付了。

**避免：** ❌ `After will finish the task...` → ✅ `After I finish the task...`（从句中用现在时表将来）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"过去完成时"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `After will finish the task...` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
