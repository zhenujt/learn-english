# Lesson 300: Book 3 Graduation / 毕业复习

> **Book 3** · Unit 12: 综合实践 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**论证结构：主张、证据、结论** — 第三册毕业综合 ⟲（从句 + 非谓语 + 段落 + 缓和） 🔄

## 📖 课文

Alex Wang proposes that we invest in observability. The primary reason is reliability. Evidence shows that three incidents could have been prevented. Admittedly, tooling cost; however, operational savings exceed cost. Therefore, we recommend recommend immediate investment.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 论证四步：主张 (claim) → 理由 (reason) → 证据 (evidence) → 结论 (conclusion)。

**示范：** `Consequently, the team decided to increase test coverage.` — 因此，团队决定提高测试覆盖率。

**避免：** ❌ `So we must change.` → ✅ `Therefore, we recommend considering this change.`（正式结论用 therefore/recommend）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"毕业复习"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `So we must change.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
