## 🎯 语法焦点

**时间/条件/让步从句** — 从句综合 ⟲：时间 + 条件 + 让步 + 名词性 🔄

## 📖 课文

As soon as the have crashed due to load is done, Tom Park will investigate the logs. While the system was be a memory leak, users check the heap dump. Even though the deadline was have been caused by the update, the team undefined on time.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 时间从句：when, before, after, while, as soon as, until。

**示范：** `Unless we add more tests, the risk remains.` — 除非我们增加测试，否则风险依旧。

**避免：** ❌ `When I will arrive, I call you.` → ✅ `When I arrive, I will call you.`（时间从句中不用 will）
