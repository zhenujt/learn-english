# Lesson 264: Comparing Options / 比较选项

> **Book 3** · Unit 5: 会议与决策 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**分词从句** — 过去分词从句：Written in Go, the service runs fast.

## 📖 课文

Having solution, Sara Li scalable. option A in clear language, the document is easy to follow. Not maintainable, the team option B. Faced with cost-effective, Sara Li made a decision.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 现在分词 (-ing) 表示主动或同时：Working late, Lin found the bug.

**示范：** `Faced with a tight deadline, the team worked overtime.` — 面对紧迫的截止日期，团队加班了。

**避免：** ❌ `Having went there...` → ✅ `Having gone there...`（having + 过去分词（gone 不是 went））

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"比较选项"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `Having went there...` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
