# Lesson 244: Cause and Result / 原因与结果从句

> **Book 3** · Unit 1: 从句建立逻辑 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**时间/条件/让步从句** — 条件从句深化：if / unless / provided that ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Before Sara Li noticed the spike, the team restarted the service. When the latency increased finishes, Sara Li will added caching. Although the team was small was tight, they handled the load. Unless we act now, the risk will grow.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 时间从句：when, before, after, while, as soon as, until。

**示范：** `Although the deadline was tight, we delivered.` — 虽然截止日期很紧，我们还是交付了。

**避免：** ❌ `When I will arrive, I call you.` → ✅ `When I arrive, I will call you.`（时间从句中不用 will）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"原因与结果从句"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `When I will arrive, I call you.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
