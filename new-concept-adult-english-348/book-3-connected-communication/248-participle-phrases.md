# Lesson 248: Participle Phrases / 分词短语

> **Book 3** · Unit 2: 修饰与信息密度 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**时间/条件/让步从句** — 让步从句：although / even though / despite ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Before Tom Park reviewed the code, the team approved the merge. When the Written in Go finishes, Tom Park will runs efficiently. Although the knowing the risk was tight, they proceeded carefully. Unless we act now, the risk will grow.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 时间从句：when, before, after, while, as soon as, until。

**示范：** `Unless we add more tests, the risk remains.` — 除非我们增加测试，否则风险依旧。

**避免：** ❌ `After will finish the task...` → ✅ `After I finish the task...`（从句中用现在时表将来）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"分词短语"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `After will finish the task...` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
