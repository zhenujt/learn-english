# Lesson 245: Review B3U1 / 复习

> **Book 3** · Unit 1: 从句建立逻辑 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**时间/条件/让步从句** — 条件从句深化：if / unless / provided that ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

As soon as the pipeline breaks is done, Alex Wang will rollback. While the system was completes the review, users merge immediately. Even though the deadline was pressure was high, the team stayed calm on time.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 时间从句：when, before, after, while, as soon as, until。

**示范：** `While the database was migrating, users saw errors.` — 数据库迁移期间用户看到了报错。

**避免：** ❌ `After will finish the task...` → ✅ `After I finish the task...`（从句中用现在时表将来）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"复习"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `After will finish the task...` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
