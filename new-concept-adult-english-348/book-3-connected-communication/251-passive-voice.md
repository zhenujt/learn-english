# Lesson 251: Passive Voice / 被动语态

> **Book 3** · Unit 3: 准确描述事件 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**名词性从句** — 名词性从句：I believe that ... / What he said ...

## 📖 课文

It is clear that the feature needs released. Lin Wei doesn't know whether the security patch will migrated. What Lin Wei suggested was to database. How we handle this matters.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** that 从句作宾语：I believe that the approach is correct.

**示范：** `I think that we should delay the release.` — 我认为我们应该推迟发布。

**避免：** ❌ `That is he late surprised me.` → ✅ `That he was late surprised me.`（主语从句需要完整结构）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"被动语态"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `That is he late surprised me.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
