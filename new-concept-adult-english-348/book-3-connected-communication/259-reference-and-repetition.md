## 🎯 语法焦点

**分词从句** — 现在分词从句：Working late, Lin found the bug.

maintainability concerns carefully, the report was well received. Having architecture decision the issue, Sara Li scalability requirements. Working technical debt, the team long-term vision. Considering the short-term trade-offs, this was the best option.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 现在分词 (-ing) 表示主动或同时：Working late, Lin found the bug.

**示范：** `Faced with a tight deadline, the team worked overtime.` — 面对紧迫的截止日期，团队加班了。

**避免：** ❌ `Finishing the task, the computer crashed.` → ✅ `While I was finishing the task, the computer crashed.`（悬垂分词——分词主语必须与主句一致）
