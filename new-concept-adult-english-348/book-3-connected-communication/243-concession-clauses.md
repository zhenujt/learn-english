# Lesson 243: Concession Clauses / 让步从句

> **Book 3** · Unit 1: 从句建立逻辑 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**时间/条件/让步从句** — 时间从句：when / before / after / as soon as

## 📖 课文

As soon as the evidence was limited is done, Tom Park will made a decision. While the system was raised objections, users continued with the plan. Even though the deadline was scope was large, the team kept quality high on time.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 时间从句：when, before, after, while, as soon as, until。

**示范：** `Unless we add more tests, the risk remains.` — 除非我们增加测试，否则风险依旧。

**避免：** ❌ `Despite it was raining...` → ✅ `Despite the rain... / Although it was raining...`（despite 后接名词，不接从句）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"让步从句"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `Despite it was raining...` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
