# Lesson 255: Review B3U3 / 复习

> **Book 3** · Unit 3: 准确描述事件 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**时间/条件/让步从句** — 从句综合 ⟲：时间 + 条件 + 让步 + 名词性 🔄

## 📖 课文

As soon as the improvement is done, Alex Wang will implemented. While the system was issue, users resolved. Even though the deadline was risk, the team mitigated on time.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 时间从句：when, before, after, while, as soon as, until。

**示范：** `While the database was migrating, users saw errors.` — 数据库迁移期间用户看到了报错。

**避免：** ❌ `Despite it was raining...` → ✅ `Despite the rain... / Although it was raining...`（despite 后接名词，不接从句）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"复习"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `Despite it was raining...` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
