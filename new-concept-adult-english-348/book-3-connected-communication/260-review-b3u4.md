# Lesson 260: Review B3U4 / 复习

> **Book 3** · Unit 4: 段落与衔接 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**分词从句** — 现在分词从句：Working late, Lin found the bug.

## 📖 课文

Having project summary, Alex Wang key achievements. lessons learned in clear language, the document is easy to follow. Not next steps, the team team acknowledgment. Faced with future direction, Alex Wang made a decision.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 现在分词 (-ing) 表示主动或同时：Working late, Lin found the bug.

**示范：** `Working remotely, I save two hours of commuting.` — 远程办公省了两小时通勤。

**避免：** ❌ `Worked in Python, it runs fast.` → ✅ `Written in Python, it runs fast.`（表被动用过去分词）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"复习"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `Worked in Python, it runs fast.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
