# Lesson 297: Risk Email / 风险邮件

> **Book 3** · Unit 12: 综合实践 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**正式缓和语气与学术表达** — 正式缓和语气：may / appear to / suggest

## 📖 课文

It is possible that the timeline will at risk. The results indicate a resource gap trend. Mia Chen believes the affect delivery might be mitigation. Further investigation may reduce impact.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 缓和语气避免绝对化：may, might, appear to, seem to, suggest, indicate。

**示范：** `This appears to be a configuration issue.` — 这似乎是一个配置问题。

**避免：** ❌ `The data shows we must change.` → ✅ `The data indicates we should consider changing.`（正式报告用缓和表达）
