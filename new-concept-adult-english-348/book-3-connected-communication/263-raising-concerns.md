# Lesson 263: Raising Concerns / 提出担忧

> **Book 3** · Unit 5: 会议与决策 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**分词从句** — 过去分词从句：Written in Go, the service runs fast.

## 📖 课文

limited carefully, the report was well received. Having raised a risk the issue, Tom Park capacity. Working critical, the team proposed mitigation. Considering the team agreed, this was the best option.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 现在分词 (-ing) 表示主动或同时：Working late, Lin found the bug.

**示范：** `Not knowing the answer, he asked his colleague.` — 由于不知道答案，他问了同事。

**避免：** ❌ `Worked in Python, it runs fast.` → ✅ `Written in Python, it runs fast.`（表被动用过去分词）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"提出担忧"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `Worked in Python, it runs fast.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
