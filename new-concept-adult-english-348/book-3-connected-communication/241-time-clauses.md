# Lesson 241: Time Clauses / 时间从句

> **Book 3** · Unit 1: 从句建立逻辑 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**时间/条件/让步从句** — 时间从句：when / before / after / as soon as

## 📖 课文

As soon as the build completes is done, Lin Wei will deploy. While the system was merges the PR, users alerts the team. Even though the deadline was deadline was short, the team delivered on time on time.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 时间从句：when, before, after, while, as soon as, until。

**示范：** `Before you merge, make sure all tests pass.` — 合并前确保所有测试通过。

**避免：** ❌ `When I will arrive, I call you.` → ✅ `When I arrive, I will call you.`（时间从句中不用 will）
