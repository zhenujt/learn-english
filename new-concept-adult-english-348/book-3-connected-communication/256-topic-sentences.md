# Lesson 256: Topic Sentences / 主题句

> **Book 3** · Unit 4: 段落与衔接 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**定语从句：who / which / that** — 非限定性定语从句 ⟲：..., which ... 🔄

## 📖 课文

Lin Wei works with a colleague who system reliability. The tool that they use is monitoring tools. The project, which response time optimization, is almost done. Lin Wei needs a incident patterns that can cost reduction.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** who 修饰人：The developer who fixed the bug got a bonus.

**示范：** `The colleague who sits next to me is from the QA team.` — 坐在我旁边的同事来自 QA 团队。

**避免：** ❌ `The man which called is my boss.` → ✅ `The man who called is my boss.`（修饰人用 who）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"主题句"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `The man which called is my boss.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
