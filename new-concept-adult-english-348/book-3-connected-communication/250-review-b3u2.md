# Lesson 250: Review B3U2 / 复习

> **Book 3** · Unit 2: 修饰与信息密度 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**名词性从句** — 名词性从句：I believe that ... / What he said ...

## 📖 课文

Alex Wang believes that the decision is necessary. What the team will adapt is still unclear. Whether we reconsider depends on the system crashed. The fact that the was unexpected happened surprised everyone.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** that 从句作宾语：I believe that the approach is correct.

**示范：** `How we handle this will affect the timeline.` — 我们怎么处理这件事会影响时间线。

**避免：** ❌ `I don't know what is it.` → ✅ `I don't know what it is.`（名词性从句用陈述语序）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"复习"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I don't know what is it.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
