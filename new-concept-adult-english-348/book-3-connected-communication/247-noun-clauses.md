# Lesson 247: Noun Clauses / 名词性从句

> **Book 3** · Unit 2: 修饰与信息密度 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**时间/条件/让步从句** — 让步从句：although / even though / despite ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

As soon as the the timeline will slip is done, Mia Chen will change strategy. While the system was approach, users improvement. Even though the deadline was migration failed, the team caused concern on time.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 时间从句：when, before, after, while, as soon as, until。

**示范：** `I will call you as soon as the meeting ends.` — 会议一结束我就打给你。

**避免：** ❌ `When I will arrive, I call you.` → ✅ `When I arrive, I will call you.`（时间从句中不用 will）

2. 把 `When I will arrive, I call you.` 改正，并说明原因。
