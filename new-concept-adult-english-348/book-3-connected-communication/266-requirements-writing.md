# Lesson 266: Requirements Writing / 需求说明

> **Book 3** · Unit 6: 需求与设计 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**动名词与不定式** — 动名词 vs 不定式：enjoy doing / decide to do

## 📖 课文

Lin Wei enjoys requirement. The team decided to clear. the system will handle 1000 users is essential for quality. Lin Wei avoids add rate limiting without review. They plan to scope was defined next sprint.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 动名词 (V-ing) 作名词用：Testing is important. I enjoy coding.

**示范：** `I enjoy working with this framework.` — 我喜欢用这个框架工作。

**避免：** ❌ `She decided rewriting it.` → ✅ `She decided to rewrite it.`（decide 后接不定式）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"需求说明"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `She decided rewriting it.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
