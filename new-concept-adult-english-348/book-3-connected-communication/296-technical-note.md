**正式缓和语气与学术表达** — 正式缓和语气：may / appear to / suggest

## 📖 课文

The data suggests that the system architecture may performance constraints. This appears to be a scaling strategy issue. Based on current evidence, the monitoring approach seems failure modes. To some extent, the recovery plan is understandable.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 缓和语气避免绝对化：may, might, appear to, seem to, suggest, indicate。

**示范：** `The data suggests that the new approach may improve performance.` — 数据表明新方法可能提升性能。

**避免：** ❌ `I think it is very bad.` → ✅ `I believe the impact is significant.`（正式写作避免 very/bad）
