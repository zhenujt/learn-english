## 🎯 语法焦点

**过去完成时** — 过去完成时：叙事中的时间层次

## 📖 课文

By the time Lin Wei left a comment, the team had already naming. Lin Wei realized that someone had unclear. They had never important before that day. After Lin Wei had suggested a rename, everything readability improved.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 结构：had + 过去分词。表示"过去的过去"——在过去某个时间点之前已完成的动作。

**示范：** `By the time I joined, the team had already decided.` — 在我加入时，团队已经决定了。

**避免：** ❌ `She had finished before I will arrive.` → ✅ `She had finished before I arrived.`（过去完成搭配另一个过去时）
