# Lesson 249: Gerunds and Infinitives / 不定式与动名词

> **Book 3** · Unit 2: 修饰与信息密度 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**时间/条件/让步从句** — 让步从句：although / even though / despite ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

As soon as the Documenting is done, Sara Li will avoid rushing. While the system was refactoring, users implement the new API. Even though the deadline was plan to release, the team considering postponing on time.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 时间从句：when, before, after, while, as soon as, until。

**示范：** `Although the deadline was tight, we delivered.` — 虽然截止日期很紧，我们还是交付了。

**避免：** ❌ `Despite it was raining...` → ✅ `Despite the rain... / Although it was raining...`（despite 后接名词，不接从句）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"不定式与动名词"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `Despite it was raining...` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
