# Lesson 290: Review B3U10 / 复习

> **Book 3** · Unit 10: 展示与影响 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**语篇衔接与正式写作** — 平行结构与信息密度 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

This report examines increase automation. First, we review efficiency. Furthermore, data supports the claim is discussed. On the other hand, initial investment. In conclusion, Alex Wang recommends ROI within six months. As mentioned earlier, propose a phased approach remains key.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 主题句：每段第一句概括该段要点。

**示范：** `As mentioned earlier, the primary concern is data security.` — 如前所述，首要关切是数据安全。

**避免：** ❌ `Like I said before...` → ✅ `As mentioned earlier, ...`（正式引用前文用 as mentioned）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"复习"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `Like I said before...` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
