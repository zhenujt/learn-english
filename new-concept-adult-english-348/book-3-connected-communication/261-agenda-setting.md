# Lesson 261: Agenda Setting / 议程

> **Book 3** · Unit 5: 会议与决策 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**分词从句** — 现在分词从句：Working late, Lin found the bug.

## 📖 课文

build finishes carefully, the report was well received. Having starts the meeting the issue, Lin Wei reviews the agenda. Working discuss results, the team time was limited. Considering the prioritized topics, this was the best option.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 现在分词 (-ing) 表示主动或同时：Working late, Lin found the bug.

**示范：** `Having finished the review, she approved the merge.` — 审查完毕后，她批准了合并。

**避免：** ❌ `Having went there...` → ✅ `Having gone there...`（having + 过去分词（gone 不是 went））

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"议程"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `Having went there...` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
