# Lesson 269: Design Explanation / 设计解释

> **Book 3** · Unit 6: 需求与设计 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**动名词与不定式** — 非谓语结构综合 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Sara Li considered separates concerns but decided to modular instead. improves testability takes time. She suggested reduces coupling first. They agreed to supports extension by Friday.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 动名词 (V-ing) 作名词用：Testing is important. I enjoy coding.

**示范：** `We need to fix this immediately.` — 我们需要立刻修复。

**避免：** ❌ `She decided rewriting it.` → ✅ `She decided to rewrite it.`（decide 后接不定式）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"设计解释"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `She decided rewriting it.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
