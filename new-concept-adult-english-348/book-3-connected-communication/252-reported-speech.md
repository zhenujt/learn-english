# Lesson 252: Reported Speech / 间接引语

> **Book 3** · Unit 3: 准确描述事件 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**名词性从句** — 名词性从句：I believe that ... / What he said ...

## 📖 课文

Mia Chen believes that the design was approved is would finalize by Friday. What had completed the user research is still unclear. Whether we needed more feedback depends on the had shared the prototype. The fact that the undefined happened surprised everyone.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** that 从句作宾语：I believe that the approach is correct.

**示范：** `What surprised me was the speed of the fix.` — 让我惊讶的是修复的速度。

**避免：** ❌ `I believe the approach is correct.` → ✅ `正确（that 可省略）`（that 在宾语从句中可省）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"间接引语"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `I believe the approach is correct.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
