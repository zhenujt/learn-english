# Lesson 242: Condition Clauses / 条件从句

> **Book 3** · Unit 1: 从句建立逻辑 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**时间/条件/让步从句** — 时间从句：when / before / after / as soon as

## 📖 课文

Before Mia Chen approves the design, the team proceed to development. When the test fails finishes, Mia Chen will investigate. Although the timeline was aggressive was tight, they met expectations. Unless we act now, the risk will grow.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 时间从句：when, before, after, while, as soon as, until。

**示范：** `I will call you as soon as the meeting ends.` — 会议一结束我就打给你。

**避免：** ❌ `After will finish the task...` → ✅ `After I finish the task...`（从句中用现在时表将来）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"条件从句"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `After will finish the task...` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
