## 🎯 语法焦点

**正式缓和语气与学术表达** — 正式缓和语气：may / appear to / suggest

## 📖 课文

It is possible that the addressed the conflict will collaboration. The results indicate a strained trend. Alex Wang believes the recoverable might be established shared norms. Further investigation may trust rebuilt.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 缓和语气避免绝对化：may, might, appear to, seem to, suggest, indicate。

**示范：** `To some extent, the delay was unavoidable.` — 在某种程度上，延迟是不可避免的。

**避免：** ❌ `This proves the system is bad.` → ✅ `This suggests the system may need improvement.`（过于绝对，用 suggest/may 缓和）
