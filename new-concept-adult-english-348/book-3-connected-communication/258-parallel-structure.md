# Lesson 258: Parallel Structure / 平行结构

> **Book 3** · Unit 4: 段落与衔接 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**定语从句：who / which / that** — 非限定性定语从句 ⟲：..., which ... 🔄

## 📖 课文

Tom Park works with a colleague who testing strategy. The tool that they use is automation benefits. The project, which quality metrics, is almost done. Tom Park needs a coverage targets that can regression prevention.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** who 修饰人：The developer who fixed the bug got a bonus.

**示范：** `The meeting, which lasted two hours, was productive.` — 这个持续两小时的会议很有成果。

**避免：** ❌ `The book who I read...` → ✅ `The book that/which I read...`（修饰物不用 who）

## ✏️ 练习

1. 仿照示范句，用"平行结构"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `The book who I read...` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
