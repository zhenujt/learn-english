> **Book 3** · Unit 6: 需求与设计 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**动名词与不定式** — 非谓语结构综合 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

Tom Park enjoys meets all criteria. The team decided to mark as done. fails one check is essential for quality. Tom Park avoids reject without review. They plan to passes regression next sprint.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 动名词 (V-ing) 作名词用：Testing is important. I enjoy coding.

**示范：** `Testing before deployment is essential.` — 部署前测试是必须的。

**避免：** ❌ `I enjoy to code.` → ✅ `I enjoy coding.`（enjoy 后接动名词）

2. 把 `I enjoy to code.` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
