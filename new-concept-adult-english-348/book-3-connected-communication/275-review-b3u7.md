# Lesson 275: Review B3U7 / 复习

> **Book 3** · Unit 7: 质量与问题 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**过去完成时** — 过去完成时：叙事中的时间层次

## 📖 课文

When Alex Wang arrived, the bug had already triaged. Alex Wang had regression before the meeting started. Had they released earlier, the result prevented. The issue had patch the previous night.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 结构：had + 过去分词。表示"过去的过去"——在过去某个时间点之前已完成的动作。

**示范：** `After he had reviewed the code, he approved the PR.` — 在审查完代码后，他批准了 PR。

**避免：** ❌ `I had went there before.` → ✅ `I had gone there before.`（go 的过去分词是 gone）

2. 把 `I had went there before.` 改正，并说明原因。
