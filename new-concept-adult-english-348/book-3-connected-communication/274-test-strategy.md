# Lesson 274: Test Strategy / 测试策略

> **Book 3** · Unit 7: 质量与问题 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**过去完成时** — 过去完成时：叙事中的时间层次

## 📖 课文

By the time Sara Li automates unit tests, the team had already catch regressions early. Sara Li realized that someone had skips integration tests. They had never miss interface bugs before that day. After Sara Li had adds E2E coverage, everything increase confidence.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 结构：had + 过去分词。表示"过去的过去"——在过去某个时间点之前已完成的动作。

**示范：** `They had never used cloud services before 2020.` — 2020 年之前他们从未用过云服务。

**避免：** ❌ `When I arrived, the meeting already started.` → ✅ `When I arrived, the meeting had already started.`（表示更早的过去用过去完成）
