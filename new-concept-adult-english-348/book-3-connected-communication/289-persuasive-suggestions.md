# Lesson 289: Persuasive Suggestions / 说服建议

> **Book 3** · Unit 10: 展示与影响 · 难度：★★★☆☆

## 🎯 语法焦点

**语篇衔接与正式写作** — 平行结构与信息密度 ⟲ 🔄

## 📖 课文

The following section addresses adopt microservices. In addition to scalability, we consider evidence shows 40% improvement. However, migration cost should not be overlooked. To summarize, the best approach is long-term savings justify it.

## 📐 本课核心

**规则：** 主题句：每段第一句概括该段要点。

**示范：** `On the other hand, the alternative requires less upfront investment.` — 另一方面，替代方案需要的前期投入更少。

**避免：** ❌ `And also we need...` → ✅ `Furthermore, we need... / In addition, ...`（正式写作避免 and also 开头）

1. 仿照示范句，用"说服建议"的真实场景写一句英语。
2. 把 `And also we need...` 改正，并说明原因。
3. 用本课语法焦点说 5 句关于你工作或生活的话，录音 60 秒。
