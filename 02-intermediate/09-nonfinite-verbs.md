# 09 Infinitives, Gerunds, and Participles

## Definition and Core Formula

Use `to + V` after verbs such as `decide` and for purpose; use `V-ing` as a noun or after a preposition; use participles to modify nouns. A non-finite form cannot be the only main verb.

## Progressive Examples

1. `I want to learn English. Reading helps.` 2. `Before deploying, back up the database.` 3. `Participants evaluated a prototype designed to reduce load.`

## Common Mistakes

`enjoy to read` -> `enjoy reading`. `decided going` -> `decided to go`. `After to finish` -> `After finishing`.

## Self-check and Answers

`We decided ___ the release.` Answer: `to delay`. `___ early saves time.` Answer: `Testing`.
