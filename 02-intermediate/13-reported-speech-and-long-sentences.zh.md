# 13 间接引语与长句组织

## 定义与核心公式

直接引语重复原话：`Ana said, "I am ready."`；间接引语报告内容：`Ana said (that) she was ready.` 当报告动词是过去时，时间通常后移一步：`am -> was`，`will -> would`，`can -> could`，`today -> that day`。事实仍有效时可不后移。

长句先搭主干，再附加从句或短语：`主句 + 关键信息 + 原因/条件/限定`。一条句子只保留一个主要观点；过长就拆句。

## 递进例句

1. `He said that he was tired.`
2. `The customer told us that the page had stopped loading after the update.`
3. `The report states that the failure occurred because a dependency, which had not been tested in production, was upgraded unexpectedly.`

## 常见错误

| 易错 | 正确 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `He said me that ...` | `He told me that ...` / `He said that ...` | `say` 不直接接人。 |
| `She asked where was I.` | `She asked where I was.` | 间接问句用陈述语序。 |
| 一个句子塞入四个 `and` | 用从句、句号或列表拆分 | 保持信息层级。 |

## 自测与答案

1. 转述：`"We will fix it tomorrow," they said.` 2. 改错：`The lead said me to wait.` 3. 将“先备份，随后部署；若失败则回滚”写成清晰两句。答案：1. `They said that they would fix it the next day.` 2. `The lead told me to wait.`
