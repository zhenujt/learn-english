# 13 Reported Speech and Long Sentences

## Definition and Core Formula

Report speech with `said/told + (that) + clause`. With a past reporting verb, time commonly shifts back: `will -> would`, `am -> was`. Build long sentences from a clear main clause, then add only necessary reasons, conditions, or modifiers.

## Progressive Examples

1. `He said that he was tired.` 2. `The customer told us that the page had stopped loading.` 3. `The report states that the failure occurred because a dependency was upgraded.`

## Common Mistakes

`He said me` -> `He told me` or `He said that`. `She asked where was I` -> `She asked where I was`.

## Self-check and Answers

Report: `We will fix it tomorrow.` Answer: `They said that they would fix it the next day.`
