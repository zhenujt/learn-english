# 11 定语从句

## 定义与核心公式

定语从句修饰前面的名词（先行词）。人用 `who/that`，物用 `which/that`，所属关系用 `whose`，地点用 `where`，时间用 `when`。限制性从句不加逗号，说明“是哪一个”；非限制性从句用逗号补充信息，通常不用 `that`。

## 递进例句

1. `The person who called you is my manager.`
2. `This is the service that sends notification emails.`
3. `The experiment, which involved 300 participants, produced statistically significant results.`

## 常见错误

| 易错 | 正确 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `The man which helped me` | `The man who helped me` | 人用 `who`。 |
| `The tool, that we use, ...` | `The tool, which we use, ...` | 非限制性从句不用 `that`。 |
| `the developer which code...` | `the developer whose code...` | 所属关系用 `whose`。 |

## 自测与答案

1. 合并：`I use a tool. The tool checks spelling.` 2. 填空：`The office ___ we met is near the station.` 3. 给“生产环境中运行的服务”写一个限制性定语从句。答案：1. `I use a tool that checks spelling.` 2. `where`。
