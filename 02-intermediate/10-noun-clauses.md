# 10 Noun Clauses

## Definition and Core Formula

A noun clause acts as a subject, object, complement, or apposition. Use `that + statement`, `whether/if + statement`, or `wh-word + statement`. Use statement word order inside the clause.

## Progressive Examples

1. `I think that you are right.` 2. `We need to know whether the API supports it.` 3. `What the results show is that testing reduces cost.`

## Common Mistakes

`I know where is he` -> `I know where he is`. Do not combine `that` and `whether`.

## Self-check and Answers

Combine: `Where is the file? I do not know.` Answer: `I do not know where the file is.`
