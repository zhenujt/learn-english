# 12 Adverbial Clauses

## Definition and Core Formula

Adverbial clauses give time, reason, condition, contrast, purpose, or result: `when`, `because`, `if/unless`, `although`, `so that`. Use present simple, not `will`, in a future time or condition clause.

## Progressive Examples

1. `I will call when I arrive.` 2. `If the test passes, we will deploy.` 3. `Although the sample was limited, the findings are useful.`

## Common Mistakes

`If it will rain` -> `If it rains`. Do not use `although` with `but`, or `because` with `so` in the same relation.

## Self-check and Answers

Rewrite with `unless`: `We will not ship it if the test does not pass.` Answer: `We will not ship it unless the test passes.`
