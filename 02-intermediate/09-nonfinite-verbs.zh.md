# 09 不定式、动名词与分词

## 定义与核心公式

非谓语动词不能单独作句子的主谓语。`to + V` 常表示目的、计划或具体动作：`plan to deploy`；`V-ing` 可作名词或介词后的宾语：`Testing matters.` / `before deploying`；过去分词常表示被动/完成：`the files uploaded yesterday`；现在分词常表示主动/进行：`the engineer working remotely`。

## 递进例句

1. `I want to learn English. Reading every day helps.`
2. `Before deploying the update, remember to back up the database.`
3. `Participants were asked to evaluate a prototype designed to reduce cognitive load.`

## 常见错误

| 易错 | 正确 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `I enjoy to read.` | `I enjoy reading.` | `enjoy` 后接动名词。 |
| `I decided going.` | `I decided to go.` | `decide` 后接不定式。 |
| `After to finish` | `After finishing` | 介词后用 `V-ing`。 |
| `Looking at the logs, the bug was found.` | `Looking at the logs, we found the bug.` | 分词短语逻辑主语应与主句主语一致。 |

## 自测与答案

1. 填空：`We decided ___ (delay) the release.` 2. `___ (test) early saves time.` 3. 改错：`The data collecting yesterday are useful.` 答案：1. `to delay`。2. `Testing`。3. `The data collected yesterday are useful.`
