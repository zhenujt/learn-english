# 03 Five Basic Sentence Patterns

## Definition and Core Formula

The patterns are `S+V`, `S+V+O`, `S+V+C`, `S+V+IO+DO`, and `S+V+O+C`. A complement describes the subject or object: `The issue is urgent`; `The change made the page faster`.

## Progressive Examples

1. `My phone rang.` 2. `The manager gave the team a deadline.` 3. `The results indicate that testing improves reliability.`

## Common Mistakes

`I explained him the problem` -> `I explained the problem to him`. `The soup tastes deliciously` -> `The soup tastes delicious`: linking verbs take adjectives.

## Self-check and Answers

Name the pattern in `The dashboard looks clean.` Answer: `S+V+C`. Change `She sent the report to us`: `She sent us the report.`
