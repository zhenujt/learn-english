# 04 Three Basic Tenses

## Definition and Core Formula

Use present simple for habits and facts: `S + V(s/es)`; past simple for finished events: `S + V-ed/past form`; future for decisions or predictions: `will + V`, and for plans: `be going to + V`.

## Progressive Examples

1. `I take the subway every day.` 2. `The monitor detected an error at 9:00.` 3. `The researchers collected data and will compare the results.`

## Common Mistakes

`He go` -> `He goes`. `Did you went?` -> `Did you go?` `will` and `did` are followed by the base verb.

## Self-check and Answers

`The app ___ yesterday, but it usually ___ well.` Answer: `crashed; runs`.
