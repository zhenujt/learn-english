# 02 Nouns, Articles, and Determiners

## Definition and Core Formula

Countable singular nouns normally need a determiner: `a/an/the/this/my + singular noun`. Use `a/an` for a non-specific item and `the` for a known item. Use no article for general plural or uncountable nouns: `Developers need feedback.`

## Progressive Examples

1. `I need a cup of coffee.` 2. `Create an issue for the error in the payment service.` 3. `The study provides evidence that feedback improves quality.`

## Common Mistakes

`I have car` -> `I have a car`. `many informations` -> `much information` or `many pieces of information`. Use `a useful tool`, because `useful` begins with a /j/ sound.

## Self-check and Answers

Complete: `Please send me ___ update when you have ___ information.` Answer: `an; more/some`.
