# 05 否定、疑问与祈使句

## 定义与核心公式

英语用助动词帮助构成否定和疑问。实义动词的一般现在/过去：否定 `S + do/does/did not + V`，一般疑问 `Do/Does/Did + S + V?`。`be` 动词直接提前或加 `not`：`Are you ready?` / `I am not ready.` 祈使句用 `V + ...`；否定祈使句用 `Don't + V`。

特殊疑问：`Wh-word + auxiliary + S + V?`，如 `Why did the build fail?`。当疑问词本身是主语时，不用 `do`：`Who called you?`

## 递进例句

1. `Do you live nearby?` / `I do not live nearby.`
2. `Why did the CI pipeline fail, and did anyone change the configuration?`
3. `To determine whether the intervention was effective, the researchers asked participants why they did not complete the task.`

## 常见错误

| 易错 | 正确 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `Why you are late?` | `Why are you late?` | `be` 提前。 |
| `He doesn't likes it.` | `He doesn't like it.` | `does` 后动词原形。 |
| `Don't to touch it.` | `Don't touch it.` | `don't + 动词原形`。 |

## 自测与答案

1. 改为疑问：`The release failed.` 2. 改错：`Where you work?` 3. 用祈使句提醒同事不要提交密钥。答案：1. `Did the release fail?` 2. `Where do you work?` 3. `Don't commit secrets.`
