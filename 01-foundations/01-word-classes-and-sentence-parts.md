# 01 Word Classes and Sentence Parts

## Definition and Core Formula

The main word classes are nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, and determiners. The minimum English sentence is `Subject + Verb`. Build phrases as `determiner + adjective + noun` and `adverb + verb`.

## Progressive Examples

1. `I work from home.` 2. `The new developer quickly fixed the login bug.` 3. `Careful review significantly reduces security risk.`

## Common Mistakes

`Is useful` -> `It is useful`: English clauses need a subject. `I very like it` -> `I like it very much`: do not use `very` directly before `like`.

## Self-check and Answers

Label the words in `Our team carefully reviewed the proposal.` Answer: `Our` determiner, `team` subject noun, `carefully` adverb, `reviewed` verb, `proposal` noun.
