# 06 代词、修饰语与比较

## 定义与核心公式

代词要与所代替的名词在人称、数上匹配：`I/me/my/mine/myself`，`they/them/their/theirs/themselves`。形容词放在名词前或连系动词后；副词常修饰动作。比较级：`A + be/V + comparative + than + B`；最高级：`the + superlative + in/of ...`。

短形容词通常 `-er/-est`：`fast, faster, fastest`；长词用 `more/most`：`more reliable`。不规则：`good/better/best`，`bad/worse/worst`。

## 递进例句

1. `My laptop is faster than yours.`
2. `This solution is more reliable, but it runs slightly more slowly.`
3. `Among the evaluated methods, the proposed approach achieved the highest accuracy.`

## 常见错误

| 易错 | 正确 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `Me and Tom finished it.` | `Tom and I finished it.` | 主语位置用主格。 |
| `more easier` | `easier` | 不重复使用两种比较形式。 |
| `He works very hard.` | 正确 | `hard` 在此是副词；不是 `hardly`。 |

## 自测与答案

1. 填空：`This task is ___ (easy) than the last one.` 2. 改错：`The best developer in our team is she.` 3. 造句比较两个工具。答案：1. `easier`。2. `... is her.` 或 `She is the best developer ...`。3. 需有 `than` 和比较级。
