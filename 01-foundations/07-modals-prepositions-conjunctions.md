# 07 Modals, Prepositions, and Conjunctions

## Definition and Core Formula

Use `can/could/may/might/must/should + base verb`. Use `at` for a time point, `on` for days, and `in` for months or enclosed spaces. Join equal ideas with `and, but, or, so`.

## Progressive Examples

1. `Can you help me?` 2. `You should update the dependency, but you must not expose the token.` 3. `The system may fail; therefore, more tests are needed.`

## Common Mistakes

`She can to code` -> `She can code`. `because ... so ...` is redundant; choose one marker.

## Self-check and Answers

Complete: `___ Monday, we meet ___ 10:00.` Answer: `On; at`. `Don't have to` means no obligation; `mustn't` means prohibition.
