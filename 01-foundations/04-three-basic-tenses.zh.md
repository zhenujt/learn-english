# 04 三大基本时态：一般现在、一般过去、一般将来

## 定义

时态把事件放在时间线上。初学者先牢固掌握三大一般时态：习惯/事实用一般现在，已结束的过去事件用一般过去，未来计划或预测用一般将来。不要见到“现在”就机械使用 `am/is/are`；普通动作需要实义动词。

## 核心公式

- 一般现在：`S + V(s/es)`；第三人称单数加 `-s/-es`：`She deploys.`
- 一般过去：`S + V-ed` 或不规则过去式：`We went.`
- 一般将来：`S + will + V`（即时决定/预测）；`S + be going to + V`（已有计划/明显迹象）。

时间提示不绝对，但很有用：`every day, usually`；`yesterday, last week, ago`；`tomorrow, next week, soon`。

## 递进例句

1. 生活口语：`I take the subway to work every day.` / `I took a taxi yesterday.` / `I will work from home tomorrow.`
2. 工作沟通：`The monitor checks the service every minute.` / `The monitor detected an error at 9:00.` / `We will deploy the fix after lunch.`
3. 学术写作：`This paper examines how code review affects quality. The researchers collected data from 120 teams and will compare the results across projects.`

## 常见错误

| 易错写法 | 正确写法 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `He go to work daily.` | `He goes to work daily.` | 一般现在时第三人称单数加 `-s`。 |
| `Did you went?` | `Did you go?` | `did` 已表达过去，动词回原形。 |
| `I will to call you.` | `I will call you.` | `will + 动词原形`。 |
| `I am work tomorrow.` | `I will work tomorrow.` / `I am working tomorrow.` | `am` 后不能直接接动词原形。 |

## 工作表达选择

`I will check it now.` 是刚决定；`I am going to check the logs after the meeting.` 是已有安排。对排期已确定的未来，也常用现在进行时：`We are releasing version 2.0 on Friday.`

## 自测

1. 填空：`The app ___ (crash) yesterday, but it usually ___ (run) well.`
2. 改错：`Will she joins the call?`
3. 用三种一般时态各写一句，主题是一次线上故障。

## 答案

1. `crashed; runs`。2. `Will she join the call?` 3. 参考：`The system normally works well. It failed yesterday. We will release a fix tomorrow.`
