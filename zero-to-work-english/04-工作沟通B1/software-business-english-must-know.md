# Core Software Business English

> 100 short, high-frequency questions and responses for software teams, divided into cross-role `(Core)` exchanges and role- or situation-specific `(As needed)` exchanges. Replace the words in `[brackets]` with details from your own work.

## How to Use This List

1. Study 10 items a day: practise active use for `(Core)` exchanges; understand `(As needed)` exchanges first, then activate the ones relevant to your work.
2. Read both the question and response aloud.
3. Replace the bracketed words with real names, dates, and tasks.
4. Review on days 1, 3, 7, and 14.
5. Each exchange includes the Aria and Jenny American English voices at `-15%` speed. Both voices provide **Natural linking** for listening practice and **No linking** for clear word-by-word shadowing.
6. `(Core)` marks high-frequency, cross-role exchanges you should be able to use actively. `(As needed)` marks role- or situation-specific exchanges to understand first and activate when relevant.

```text
Yesterday, I worked on [the login feature].
I finished [the API changes].

Today, I am working on [the user interface].
I am also testing [the new login flow].

My next step is to [open a PR].
There are no blockers at the moment.
I expect to finish it by [Friday].
That is all from me.
```

When you have a blocker, replace the blocker sentence with:

```text
I am currently blocked by [an API issue].
I am waiting for [the backend team] to [fix the issue].
There is a risk of delay.
```

```text
Today, I am going to show you [the new login feature].
First, let me give you some background.

Previously, users had to [enter their password every time].
With this change, users can now [sign in with a code].

Let me show you how it works.
First, I [open the login page].

When I click [this button], the system [sends a code].
As you can see, [the user is now logged in].

That is the main flow.
Do you have any questions?
```

- **That is the end of the demo.**
