# 35 比较与选择：哪个方案更合适
## 本课能做什么
能比较两个选项，并说明选择理由。
## 核心公式
`more/less + 形容词 + than`；`as ... as`；`prefer A because ...`。
## 递进例句
1. `This route is shorter than that one.` 2. `The new version is as stable as the old version.` 3. `We prefer this option because it is less expensive and easier to maintain.`
## 常见错误
`more cheaper` -> `cheaper`。`as fast than` -> `as fast as`。
## 口语与写作任务
比较两个工具；写 70 词推荐意见。
## 自测与答案
“这个方案没有那个复杂。”：`This option is less complex than that one.`
