# 40 规则与流程：先做什么，再做什么
## 本课能做什么
能按顺序说明基础流程和规则。
## 核心公式
顺序词：`first, then, next, finally`；被动初步：`The form is submitted.`
## 递进例句
1. `First, open the page.` 2. `Then, enter your email address.` 3. `Finally, the request is reviewed by the support team.`
## 常见错误
`First you open, then you are submit` -> `First, open ... Then, submit ...`
## 口语与写作任务
讲解一个日常应用流程；写 70 词操作说明。
## 自测与答案
补全：`First ..., ___ ..., finally ...`：`then/next`。
