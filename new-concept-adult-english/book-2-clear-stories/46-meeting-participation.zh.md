# 46 会议参与：听不懂也能继续
## 本课能做什么
能请求重复、确认理解、简短加入讨论。
## 核心公式
`Could you repeat that?`；`If I understand correctly, ...`；`I'd like to add ...`
## 递进例句
1. `Could you repeat that, please?` 2. `If I understand correctly, we need more tests.` 3. `I'd like to add that the customer has a different requirement.`
## 常见错误
`I don't understand you` 语气生硬 -> `Could you explain that again?`
## 口语与写作任务
模拟三句会议发言；写一条会后确认。
## 自测与答案
“如果我理解正确……”：`If I understand correctly, ...`
