# 26 过去进行时：当时正在发生什么
## 本课能做什么
能描述过去某时正在进行的动作，并交代一个事件何时发生。
## 核心公式
`was/were + V-ing`；常与 `when` 连用：`I was working when he called.`
## 递进例句
1. `I was cooking at seven.` 2. `We were discussing the plan when the call ended.` 3. `The service was processing requests when the network failed.`
## 常见错误
`I was work` -> `I was working.` `When I was arrive` -> `When I arrived`。
## 口语与写作任务
说一个意外发生时你正在做的事；写 50 词事件经过。
## 自测与答案
`They ___ (test) when the error appeared.`：`were testing`。
