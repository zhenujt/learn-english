# 44 复盘一次问题：发生了什么
## 本课能做什么
能按时间线说明一次小故障、影响和恢复。
## 核心公式
`At + 时间, ...`；`because of + 名词`；`was fixed by + 时间`。
## 递进例句
1. `The page stopped working.` 2. `At 10:00, users could not log in.` 3. `The problem was fixed by noon after the team corrected the configuration.`
## 常见错误
`because configuration wrong` -> `because the configuration was wrong.`
## 口语与写作任务
讲一个简单故障；写 100 词事件时间线。
## 自测与答案
“问题在中午前解决了。”：`The problem was fixed by noon.`
