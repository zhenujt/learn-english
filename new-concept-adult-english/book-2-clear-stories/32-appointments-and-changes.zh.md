# 32 预约与变更：改时间和取消
## 本课能做什么
能确认、改期或取消预约，同时保持礼貌。
## 核心公式
`I'd like to reschedule/cancel ...`；`Could we move it to + 时间?`
## 递进例句
1. `I'd like to cancel my appointment.` 2. `Could we move the call to Friday?` 3. `I need to reschedule because I have a conflict at that time.`
## 常见错误
`change the meeting to Friday?` 太直接 -> `Could we move the meeting to Friday?`
## 口语与写作任务
模拟改一次预约；写 60 词改期邮件。
## 自测与答案
“我们能把会议改到明天吗？”：`Could we move the meeting to tomorrow?`
