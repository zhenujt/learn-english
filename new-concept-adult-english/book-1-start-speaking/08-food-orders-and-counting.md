# 08 Food, Orders, and Counting

## What You Can Do

Order food and drinks, say what you do not want, and ask for quantities.

## Minimum Useful Sentences

`I'd like a coffee, please.` `Can I have two bottles of water?` `No sugar, please.`

## Core Formula

Use `I'd like + noun` and `Can I have + noun?`. Count countable nouns directly; use a container for uncountable nouns: `two bottles of water`. Use `any` in most questions and negatives.

## Progressive Examples

1. `I'd like some water.` 2. `Can I have a sandwich and a cup of tea?` 3. `Could I have two bottles of water for the meeting room?`

## Common Mistakes

`two water` -> `two bottles of water`. `I don't have some money` -> `I don't have any money`.

## Workplace Practice

`We need six cups of coffee and some water for the meeting.`

## Self-check and Answers

`Do you have (some/any) coffee?` Answer: `any`. “请给我两瓶水。” Answer: `Can I have two bottles of water, please?`
