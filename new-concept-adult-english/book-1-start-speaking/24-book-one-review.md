# 24 Book One Review
## Goal and Core Formula
Review: `I am` for identity/state, present simple for habits, present continuous for now, and past/future forms for completed and planned actions.
## Examples
`I am a developer.` `I am testing a feature now.` `I found an error yesterday and will share an update tomorrow.`
## Common Mistakes
`I am finished` is not usually “I completed it”; say `I finished`.
## Practice and Answers
Record a 90-second mock stand-up and write 80--100 words. Check each clause has a subject and verb.
