# 09 Shopping, Prices, and Choices

## What You Can Do

Ask about prices and sizes, make a choice, and compare simple options.

## Minimum Useful Sentences

`How much is this?` `Do you have a larger size?` `I'll take it.`

## Core Formula

Use `How much is + singular?` and `How much are + plural?`. Use `Do you have + noun?` for availability. Say `I'll take it` to buy something.

## Progressive Examples

1. `How much is this T-shirt?` 2. `Do you have these shoes in a larger size?` 3. `This option is cheaper, but the other one is more reliable.`

## Common Mistakes

`How much are this?` -> `How much is this?` `I'll takes this` -> `I'll take this`.

## Workplace Practice

`Could you confirm the price for this plan?` `This plan is cheaper, but it has fewer users.`

## Self-check and Answers

`How much ___ those bags?` Answer: `are`. Translate: “你们有黑色的吗？” Answer: `Do you have it in black?`
