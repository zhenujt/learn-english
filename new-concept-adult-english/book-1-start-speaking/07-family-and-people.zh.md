# 07 家庭与人物：介绍身边的人

## 本课能做什么

能介绍家庭成员、同事和朋友，并用简单形容词描述人。

## 先会说：最小可用句

`This is my sister.` `She is a teacher.` `She is very kind.`

## 发音与节奏

`this` 的开头轻咬舌尖；不要读成 `zis`。`she's` 是 `she is` 的自然缩略。

## 核心词与短语

`mother, father, husband, wife, colleague, friend, kind, busy, helpful, quiet`。用 `This is my ...` 介绍人；用 `He/She is ...` 补充身份或特点。

## 语法焦点与核心公式

`This is + 单数人/物`；`These are + 复数人/物`。  
`He/She is + 身份/形容词`。  
所有格：`my, your, his, her, our, their + 名词`，如 `her team`。

## 递进例句

1. `This is my brother. He is kind.`
2. `These are my colleagues. They are on the product team.`
3. `My manager is helpful, and her feedback is clear.`

## 原创对话

**Bo:** Who is she?  
**Mei:** She is my colleague, Anna. She is a designer.  
**Bo:** Is she on your team?  
**Mei:** Yes, she is.

## 工作场景

介绍同事时先给姓名和角色：`This is David. He is our QA engineer.` 不确定性别或不想强调性别时可用：`This is Alex. They are on our team.`

## 常见错误

| 易错 | 正确 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `She is my colleague, he is kind.` | `She is my colleague. She is kind.` | 代词与人物性别/指代一致。 |
| `This are my parents.` | `These are my parents.` | 复数用 `these are`。 |
| `He is engineer.` | `He is an engineer.` | 单数可数职业前需要冠词。 |

## 听读、口语与写作任务

朗读：`This is my colleague. She is a helpful engineer.` 用三句介绍一位真实或虚构同事。写 35--50 词，介绍一个团队成员及其工作。

## 自测

1. 填空：`___ are my friends.` 2. 改错：`Her is a designer.` 3. 翻译：这是我们的产品经理。4. 用 `their` 写一句。

## 答案与自查

1. `These`。2. `She is a designer.` 3. `This is our product manager.` 4. `their` 后必须有名词，如 `Their office is near here.`

## 课后复习卡

`This is ...`；`These are ...`；`He/She is ...`；`my/our/their`；`a/an + 职业`。
