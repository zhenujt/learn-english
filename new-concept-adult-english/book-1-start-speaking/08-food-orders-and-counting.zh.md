# 08 饮食与点单：我想要什么？

## 本课能做什么

能在咖啡店或餐厅点单，表达想要、不想要和数量。

## 先会说：最小可用句

`I'd like a coffee, please.` `Can I have two bottles of water?` `No sugar, please.`

## 发音与节奏

`I'd like` 连成一个节奏，不要分成 `I would like`。`water` 在不同口音中不同，先保证词重音和语气自然。

## 核心词与短语

`menu, coffee, tea, water, rice, chicken, bottle, cup, some, any, please`。礼貌点单首选 `I'd like ...`，比 `I want ...` 更温和。

## 语法焦点与核心公式

`I'd like + 名词`：`I'd like a tea.`  
`Can I have + 名词?`：`Can I have some water?`  
可数：`a cup / two cups`；不可数：`some water / a bottle of water`。否定和问句里常用 `any`：`Do you have any tea?`

## 递进例句

1. `I'd like some water, please.`
2. `Can I have a chicken sandwich and a cup of tea?`
3. `Could I have two bottles of water with no ice for the meeting room?`

## 原创对话

**Staff:** What would you like?  
**Lin:** I'd like a coffee and a sandwich, please.  
**Staff:** Anything else?  
**Lin:** No, thank you.

## 工作场景

为会议订饮料：`We need six cups of coffee and some water for the meeting.` 询问限制：`Does anyone have any food allergies?` 不要把不可数 `water` 说成 `waters`，除非指不同品牌/种类。

## 常见错误

| 易错 | 正确 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `I want a coffee, please.` | `I'd like a coffee, please.` | 前者不一定错，但后者更礼貌。 |
| `two water` | `two bottles of water` | `water` 通常不可数。 |
| `I don't have some money.` | `I don't have any money.` | 否定句常用 `any`。 |

## 听读、口语与写作任务

模仿点单对话三遍。口头为两个人点一份早餐。写一条 35 词的会议茶歇清单，至少包含两个可数名词和一个不可数名词。

## 自测

1. 填空：`I'd like ___ cup of tea.` 2. 选择：`Do you have (some/any) coffee?` 3. 翻译：请给我两瓶水。4. 礼貌拒绝追加点单。

## 答案与自查

1. `a`。2. `any`。3. `Can I have two bottles of water, please?` 4. `No, thank you.`

## 课后复习卡

`I'd like ...`；`Can I have ...?`；`some/any`；`a bottle of ...`；`Anything else?`
