# 06 Places and Directions

## What You Can Do

Ask where a place is and give simple directions in an office, station, or building.

## Minimum Useful Sentences

`Where is the meeting room?` `It's on the second floor.` `Go straight and turn left.`

## Core Formula

Use `Where is + singular?` and `Where are + plural?` Answer with `It is + preposition phrase`. Say `Go straight` and `Turn left/right` for directions.

## Progressive Examples

1. `Where is the restroom?` 2. `The café is next to the subway station.` 3. `The training room is on the fifth floor, opposite the lift.`

## Common Mistakes

`Where the room is?` -> `Where is the room?` `in the second floor` -> `on the second floor`.

## Workplace Practice

`Where are you? Are you in the meeting room or online?` `I'm at my desk on the third floor.`

## Self-check and Answers

`The office is ___ the second floor.` Answer: `on`. Translate: “请直走，然后左转。” Answer: `Please go straight and then turn left.`
