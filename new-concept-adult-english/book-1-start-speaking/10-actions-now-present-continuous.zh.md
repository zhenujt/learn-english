# 10 正在发生的事：现在进行时

## 本课能做什么

能说正在做什么、询问对方当前状态，并在工作中给出简短进度。

## 先会说：最小可用句

`I'm working now.` `What are you doing?` `We're testing the new version.`

## 发音与节奏

`I'm working` 中 `I'm` 很短，重读 `work`。`-ing` 结尾清楚即可，不要额外加“格”。

## 核心词与短语

`working, cooking, waiting, studying, testing, checking, fixing, right now, at the moment`。

## 语法焦点与核心公式

`S + am/is/are + V-ing`。  
`What + am/is/are + S + V-ing?`  
拼写：`make -> making`；`run -> running`；`study -> studying`。进行时强调“此刻/这段时间正在发生”，不是日常习惯。

## 递进例句

1. `I'm cooking dinner now.`
2. `She is waiting for the bus at the moment.`
3. `We are testing the payment flow, so the service may be slow for a few minutes.`

## 原创对话

**Kai:** Hi, what are you doing?  
**Rui:** I'm checking the test report.  
**Kai:** Are you free now?  
**Rui:** Not now. I'm joining a call in five minutes.

## 工作场景

状态更新用现在进行时：`I'm investigating the error.` 团队状态：`We are deploying a small fix.` 区分习惯：`I work from home on Fridays.` 与现在：`I'm working from home today.`

## 常见错误

| 易错 | 正确 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `I working now.` | `I'm working now.` | 进行时必须有 `am/is/are`。 |
| `She is work now.` | `She is working now.` | `be + V-ing`。 |
| `I am usually working at nine.` | `I usually work at nine.` | 习惯用一般现在时。 |

## 听读、口语与写作任务

看周围说三句正在发生的事。录音回答 `What are you doing now?`，至少 20 秒。写一条 35--50 词工作状态，包含正在做的事、原因和下一步。

## 自测

1. 填空：`They ___ (test) the app now.` 2. 改错：`What you are doing?` 3. 翻译：我正在等你的回复。4. 区分：写一句习惯和一句当前状态。

## 答案与自查

1. `are testing`。2. `What are you doing?` 3. `I'm waiting for your reply.` 4. 示例：`I check email every morning. I'm checking email now.`

## 课后复习卡

`I'm + V-ing`；`What are you doing?`；`right now`；`at the moment`；`We're testing ...`
