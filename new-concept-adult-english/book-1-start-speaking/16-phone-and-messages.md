# 16 Phone Calls and Messages
## Goal and Core Formula
`This is + name.` `May I speak to + person?` `Can I take a message?`
## Examples
`Hello, this is Lin.` `He is not available. Can I take a message?`
## Common Mistakes
`Can you repeat again?` -> `Can you repeat that?`
## Practice and Answers
“我可以留言吗？” Answer: `Can I leave a message?`
