# 06 地点与方向：在哪里？怎么走？

## 本课能做什么

能问地点、描述常见位置，并在办公楼、地铁站或陌生园区请求简单指路。

## 先会说：最小可用句

`Where is the meeting room?` `It's on the second floor.` `Go straight and turn left.`

## 发音与节奏

- `where` 和 `were` 发音不同；`Where is ...?` 要把 `where` 重读。
- `floor` 的 /r/ 不必夸张，但不要读成中文“佛楼”。

## 核心词与短语

| 英语 | 含义 | 立即使用 |
|---|---|---|
| where | 在哪里 | `Where is ...?` |
| next to | 紧挨着 | `next to the lift` |
| opposite | 在对面 | `opposite the bank` |
| floor | 楼层 | `on the third floor` |
| lift / elevator | 电梯 | `Take the lift.` |
| straight / left / right | 直走 / 左 / 右 | `Turn right.` |

## 语法焦点与核心公式

`Where is + 单数地点/物品?`；`Where are + 复数?`  
`It is + 介词短语`：`It is next to the lift.`  
`Go + 副词`：`Go straight.`；`Turn + left/right.`

## 递进例句

1. `Where is the restroom? It's over there.`
2. `The café is next to the subway station.`
3. `The training room is on the fifth floor, opposite the main lift.`

## 原创对话

**Jun:** Excuse me. Where is Room 503?  
**Sara:** Go straight. It is on the fifth floor, next to the lift.  
**Jun:** Thank you.  
**Sara:** You're welcome.

## 工作场景

远程会议中不要说 `Where you?`。可以说：`Hi, where are you? Are you in the meeting room or online?` 说明位置：`I'm at my desk, on the third floor.`

## 常见错误

| 易错 | 正确 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `Where the room is?` | `Where is the room?` | 普通疑问句中 `is` 在主语前。 |
| `It is in second floor.` | `It is on the second floor.` | 楼层通常用 `on`。 |
| `Go to straight.` | `Go straight.` | `straight` 此处是副词，不用 `to`。 |

## 听读、口语与写作任务

读三遍：`Go straight, turn right, and take the lift to the third floor.` 口头说明从你家到最近地铁站的两步路线。写 30--40 词，告诉新同事会议室在哪里。

## 自测

1. 填空：`The office is ___ the second floor.` 2. 改错：`Where is the lifts?` 3. 翻译：请直走，然后左转。4. 用 `next to` 写一句。

## 答案与自查

1. `on`。2. `Where are the lifts?` 或 `Where is the lift?` 3. `Please go straight and then turn left.` 4. 句子必须有 `is/are`。

## 课后复习卡

`Where is ...?`；`on the ... floor`；`next to`；`Go straight.`；`Turn left/right.`
