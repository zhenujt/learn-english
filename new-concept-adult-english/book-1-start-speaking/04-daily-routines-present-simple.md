# 04 What I Do Every Day: Present Simple and Routine

## Goal

Describe routines, regular work duties, and fixed schedules. Ask what another person usually does.

## Minimum Useful Sentence

`I start work at nine. She checks the dashboard every morning.`

## Core Vocabulary

`start`, `finish`, `check`, `reply`, `join`, `usually`, `often`, `sometimes`, `every day`, `at`.

## Grammar: Present Simple

### Core Formula

`I/You/We/They + base verb`  
`He/She/It + verb-s/es`  
`Do/Does + subject + base verb?`  
`Subject + do/does not + base verb`

Use the present simple for habits, regular duties, schedules, and general facts. Use `-s/-es` after `he`, `she`, or `it`.

## Progressive Examples

1. `I usually eat breakfast at home.`
2. `Our team checks new tickets every morning, and my manager replies quickly.`
3. `The monitoring system records activity every minute and sends an alert when an error occurs.`

## Dialogue

**A:** What time do you start work?  
**B:** I usually start at nine.  
**A:** Do you check messages first?  
**B:** Yes. I check email and the project board.  
**A:** What does your team do in the afternoon?  
**B:** We test new features and join a short meeting.

## Common Mistakes

`She check` -> `She checks`.  
`Does he works?` -> `Does he work?`  
`I am usually start` -> `I usually start`.  
`He don't reply` -> `He doesn't reply`.

## Speaking and Writing

Say six sentences about a normal workday, then add one sentence about a colleague. Write 60--80 words and use `usually`, `often`, and `sometimes`.

## Self-check and Answers

1. `My colleague ___ at 8:30.` Answer: `starts`.  
2. `We ___ production on weekends.` Answer: `don't check`.  
3. Question: `She joins the daily meeting.` Answer: `Does she join the daily meeting?`  
4. Translate: “系统每天晚上发送一份报告。” Answer: `The system sends a report every evening.`
