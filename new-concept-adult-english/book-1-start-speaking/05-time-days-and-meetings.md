# 05 Time, Days, and Meetings

## What You Can Do

Ask and tell the time, say when you are free, and arrange a simple meeting.

## Minimum Useful Sentences

`What time is it?` `It's half past nine.` `Are you free on Friday?` `Let's meet at two.`

## Core Formula

Use `at + time`, `on + day/date`, and `in + month/year/part of the day`. Use `Let's + base verb` to make a suggestion: `Let's meet at two.`

## Progressive Examples

1. `It's seven o'clock.` 2. `Let's meet at the coffee shop at three.` 3. `Are you available for a 15-minute call at 10:30 on Thursday?`

## Common Mistakes

`free in Monday` -> `free on Monday`. `Let's to meet` -> `Let's meet`. `I have free` -> `I am free`.

## Workplace Practice

`Are you available at 3:00 p.m. on Thursday for a short API review?`  
`I am in another meeting at 3:00. Can we meet at 4:00 instead?`

## Self-check and Answers

Complete: `We meet ___ 9:00 ___ Monday.` Answer: `at; on`. Translate: “你明天上午有空吗？” Answer: `Are you free tomorrow morning?`
