# 14 Future Plans
## Goal and Core Formula
Use `be going to + verb` for plans and `will + base verb` for decisions, promises, and predictions.
## Examples
`I'm going to study tonight.` `I will send it now.`
## Common Mistakes
`I will to call` -> `I will call.` `I going to work` -> `I am going to work.`
## Practice and Answers
“我现在就发给你。” Answer: `I will send it to you now.`
