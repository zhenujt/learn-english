# 22 Simple Problems and Solutions
## Goal and Core Formula
Use `There is/are`, `I can't + verb`, and `I tried + V-ing` to report a problem.
## Examples
`There is an error in the login page.` `I tried restarting the app.`
## Common Mistakes
`There have an error` -> `There is an error.`
## Practice and Answers
`There ___ two errors.` Answer: `are`.
