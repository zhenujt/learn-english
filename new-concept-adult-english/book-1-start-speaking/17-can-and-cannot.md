# 17 Can and Cannot
## Goal and Core Formula
Use `can/can't + base verb` for ability, permission, and rules.
## Examples
`I can swim, but I can't drive.` `Can I open this file?`
## Common Mistakes
`I can to swim` -> `I can swim.` `He cans code` -> `He can code.`
## Practice and Answers
`She ___ use the tool.` Answer: `can`.
