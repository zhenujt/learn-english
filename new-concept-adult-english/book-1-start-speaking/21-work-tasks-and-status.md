# 21 Work Tasks and Status
## Goal and Core Formula
Use present continuous for now, simple past for completion, and `will` for next steps.
## Examples
`I'm checking the file.` `I finished the test.` `I will share an update before five.`
## Common Mistakes
`I am finish` -> `I finished` or `I am finishing`.
## Practice and Answers
Write a 50-word status update using all three time frames.
