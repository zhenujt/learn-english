# 02 Who I Am: Be, Identity, and Role

## Goal

Say who a person is, what something is, where it is, and what state it is in.

## Minimum Useful Sentence

`I am a developer. You are my colleague. She is in Beijing.`

## Grammar: Present Forms of Be

### Core Formula

`I am` / `you, we, they are` / `he, she, it is`  
`S + be + identity, adjective, or place`

Examples: `He is an engineer.` `The server is busy.` `We are in a meeting.` Use `be + not` for negatives and move `be` before the subject for questions.

## Progressive Examples

1. `I am tired, but happy.`
2. `We are on the support team. Our manager is in a meeting.`
3. `The application is unavailable because the database server is under maintenance.`

## Dialogue

**A:** Are you new here?  
**B:** Yes, I am. I'm Zhou Min, a QA engineer.  
**A:** Welcome. I'm David. I'm on the platform team.  
**B:** Is the team remote?  
**A:** Yes, it is.

## Common Mistakes

`I is` -> `I am`. `She are` -> `She is`. `I am work from home` -> `I work from home`; do not put `am` before an ordinary base verb.

## Self-check and Answers

1. `They ___ in the office.` Answer: `are`.  
2. Question: `The server is ready.` Answer: `Is the server ready?`  
3. Translate “她是我们的项目经理吗？” Answer: `Is she our project manager?`
