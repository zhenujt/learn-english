# 19 Comparisons and Preferences
## Goal and Core Formula
`prefer A to B`; `A is comparative than B`; `the + superlative`.
## Examples
`I prefer tea to coffee.` `This option is more useful.`
## Common Mistakes
`more faster` -> `faster`. `prefer tea than coffee` -> `prefer tea to coffee`.
## Practice and Answers
`This task is ___ than the last one.` Answer: `easier`.
