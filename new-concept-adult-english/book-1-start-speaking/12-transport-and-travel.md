# 12 Transport and Travel
## Goal and Core Formula
Use `by + transport`: `by bus`; ask `How do you get to ...?`; use `It takes + person + time + to + verb`.
## Examples
`I go to work by subway.` `It takes me forty minutes to get there.`
## Common Mistakes
`by the bus` -> `by bus`. `I go by walk` -> `I walk`.
## Practice and Answers
Explain your route. `We go there ___ taxi.` Answer: `by`.
