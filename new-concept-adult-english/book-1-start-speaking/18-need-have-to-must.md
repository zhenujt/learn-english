# 18 Need, Have To, and Must
## Goal and Core Formula
Use `need to`, `have to`, and `must + verb`. `Don't have to` means no need; `mustn't` means prohibited.
## Examples
`We have to finish today.` `You mustn't share customer data.`
## Common Mistakes
`must to finish` -> `must finish`.
## Practice and Answers
“你不必今天完成。” Answer: `You don't have to finish today.`
