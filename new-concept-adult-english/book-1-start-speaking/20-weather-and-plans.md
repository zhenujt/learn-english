# 20 Weather and Plans
## Goal and Core Formula
Use `It is + weather adjective` and `It is + V-ing`; connect reason with `because`.
## Examples
`It is cold today.` `We will stay inside because it is raining.`
## Common Mistakes
`Is raining` -> `It is raining.`
## Practice and Answers
“明天会很热。” Answer: `It will be hot tomorrow.`
