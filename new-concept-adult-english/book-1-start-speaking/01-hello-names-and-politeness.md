# 01 Hello: Names and Politeness

## Goal

Open a first meeting in 20--40 seconds: greet someone, say your name, ask for a name, respond politely, and close the exchange.

## Minimum Useful Sentence

`Hello. I'm Li Ming. Nice to meet you.`

## Pronunciation

Say `I'm` as one syllable, not `I-am`. In `Nice to meet you`, stress `nice` and `meet`; make `to` light.

## Core Vocabulary

`hello`, `hi`, `good morning`, `name`, `nice to meet you`, `too`, `thank you`, `see you`.

## Grammar: Be in Introductions

### Core Formula

`I'm + name/role`  
`My name is + name`  
`What's your name?`

`I'm` is the spoken short form of `I am`. Use a full question, especially when learning: `What's your name?`

## Progressive Examples

1. `Hi, I'm Wang Lei.`
2. `Good morning. My name is Wang Lei. Nice to meet you.`
3. `Hello, everyone. I'm Wang Lei from the mobile team. Thank you for joining the call.`

## Dialogue

**Mia:** Hello. I'm Mia. What's your name?  
**Chen:** Hi, I'm Chen Yu. Nice to meet you.  
**Mia:** Nice to meet you, too. Are you on the web team?  
**Chen:** Yes, I am. Thank you.

Read both roles, then replace the name and team with real information.

## Common Mistakes

`My name Wang Lei` -> `My name is Wang Lei.`  
`Nice meet you` -> `Nice to meet you.`  
`Hello teacher` -> `Hello, Ms. Zhang` or `Hello, everyone.`

## Speaking and Writing

Record three introductions: name only; name plus city; name plus role. Then write 25--35 words for a meeting opening.

## Self-check and Answers

1. `Hello. ___ Li Hua.` Answer: `I'm` or `I am`.  
2. `Nice to meet you, ___.` Answer: `too`.  
3. Translate: “大家早上好。我是陈琳，来自上海。” Answer: `Good morning, everyone. I'm Chen Lin from Shanghai.`
