# 07 Family and People

## What You Can Do

Introduce family members and colleagues, and describe them with simple adjectives.

## Minimum Useful Sentences

`This is my sister.` `She is a teacher.` `She is very kind.`

## Core Formula

Use `This is + singular` and `These are + plural`. Use `He/She is + job/adjective`. Put `my, your, his, her, our, their` before a noun.

## Progressive Examples

1. `This is my brother. He is kind.` 2. `These are my colleagues.` 3. `My manager is helpful, and her feedback is clear.`

## Common Mistakes

`This are my parents` -> `These are my parents`. `He is engineer` -> `He is an engineer`.

## Workplace Practice

`This is David. He is our QA engineer.` When a gendered pronoun is not appropriate: `This is Alex. They are on our team.`

## Self-check and Answers

`___ are my friends.` Answer: `These`. Translate: “这是我们的产品经理。” Answer: `This is our product manager.`
