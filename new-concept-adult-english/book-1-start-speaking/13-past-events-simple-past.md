# 13 Past Events: Simple Past
## Goal and Core Formula
Use `subject + past verb + past time`. Use `did + base verb` for questions and negatives.
## Examples
`I watched a film last night.` `We finished the test yesterday.`
## Common Mistakes
`Did you went?` -> `Did you go?` `I didn't finished` -> `I didn't finish.`
## Practice and Answers
Describe yesterday. `She ___ the report.` Answer: `finished`.
