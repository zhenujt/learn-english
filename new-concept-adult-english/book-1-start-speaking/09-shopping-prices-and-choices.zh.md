# 09 购物、价格与选择：这个多少钱？

## 本课能做什么

能询问价格、尺寸和选择，并完成一段简短购物对话。

## 先会说：最小可用句

`How much is this?` `Do you have a larger size?` `I'll take it.`

## 发音与节奏

`How much` 连读时重点落在 `much`。金额先清楚说数字，例如 `twenty yuan`，不要为了快而吞掉重音。

## 核心词与短语

`price, cheap, expensive, size, small, large, try on, card, cash, take`。`take` 在购物时表示“买下/要这个”。

## 语法焦点与核心公式

`How much is + 单数?`；`How much are + 复数?`  
`Do you have + 名词?`  
`I will / I'll + 动词`：`I'll take it.`  
选择时用 `this/that`（单数）和 `these/those`（复数）。

## 递进例句

1. `How much is this T-shirt?`
2. `Do you have these shoes in a larger size?`
3. `This option is cheaper, but the other one is more reliable for daily use.`

## 原创对话

**Customer:** How much are these headphones?  
**Staff:** They are 199 yuan.  
**Customer:** Do you have them in black?  
**Staff:** Yes, we do.  
**Customer:** Great. I'll take them.

## 工作场景

比较工具或服务时也可用比较句：`This plan is cheaper, but it has fewer users.` 需要确认价格时写：`Could you confirm the price for this plan?`

## 常见错误

| 易错 | 正确 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| `How much are this?` | `How much is this?` | `this` 是单数。 |
| `I take it.` | `I'll take it.` | 购买决定常用 `will`。 |
| `Do you have it black?` | `Do you have it in black?` | 颜色前常用 `in`。 |

## 听读、口语与写作任务

读对话三遍。用三个句子比较两件商品。写一条 30--45 词的采购询价消息，问价格、交付时间和一个可选项。

## 自测

1. 填空：`How much ___ those bags?` 2. 改错：`I'll takes this one.` 3. 翻译：你们有黑色的吗？4. 用 `cheaper than` 造句。

## 答案与自查

1. `are`。2. `I'll take this one.` 3. `Do you have it in black?` 4. 要有两个比较对象。

## 课后复习卡

`How much is/are ...?`；`Do you have ...?`；`in black`；`I'll take it.`；`cheaper than`。
