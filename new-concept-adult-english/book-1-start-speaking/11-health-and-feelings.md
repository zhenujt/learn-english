# 11 Health and Feelings
## Goal and Core Formula
Say how you feel: `I feel + adjective`; `I have + a/an symptom`; `I need to + verb`.
## Examples
`I feel tired today.` `I have a headache, so I need to rest.` `I am not feeling well, so I may leave the call early.`
## Common Mistakes
`I am feel tired` -> `I feel tired.` `He have a cold` -> `He has a cold.`
## Practice and Answers
Write a 30-word absence message. `She ___ tired.` Answer: `feels`.
