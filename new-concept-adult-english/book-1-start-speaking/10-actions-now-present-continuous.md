# 10 Actions Now: Present Continuous

## What You Can Do

Say what is happening now and give a short work-progress update.

## Minimum Useful Sentences

`I'm working now.` `What are you doing?` `We're testing the new version.`

## Core Formula

Use `subject + am/is/are + V-ing` for an action happening now or around now. Ask `What + am/is/are + subject + V-ing?`.

## Progressive Examples

1. `I'm cooking dinner now.` 2. `She is waiting for the bus.` 3. `We are testing the payment flow, so the service may be slow.`

## Common Mistakes

`I working now` -> `I'm working now`. `She is work` -> `She is working`. Use present simple, not continuous, for habits.

## Workplace Practice

`I'm investigating the error.` `We are deploying a small fix.` Compare: `I work from home on Fridays` and `I'm working from home today`.

## Self-check and Answers

`They ___ the app now.` Answer: `are testing`. “我正在等你的回复。” Answer: `I'm waiting for your reply.`
