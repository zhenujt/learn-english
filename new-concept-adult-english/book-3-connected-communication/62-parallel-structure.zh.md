# 62 平行结构：并列要同形
## 本课能做什么
能写清楚的列表、目标和职责。
## 核心公式
并列保持同形：`to plan, to build, and to test` 或 `planning, building, and testing`。
## 递进例句
1. `I like reading and walking.` 2. `We need to plan, build, and test.` 3. `The role includes analyzing data, writing reports, and communicating results.`
## 常见错误
`reading, to write, and coding` -> 统一形式。
## 口语与写作任务
列出三项职责；写 60 词岗位说明。
## 自测与答案
改错：`to test, fixing, and deploy` -> `to test, to fix, and to deploy`。
